# Codex round-4 findings — frozen version `442cc4b7da691ca0`

Review date: 7 September 2026. `python3 freeze.py` returned the requested hash. I read the 22 manifest files in order and treated only that set as normative. I did not read another round-4 review before completing this file, and neither implementation imports or copies a review-folder simulation.

## Overall assessment

**Needs revision before Stage 0A implementation.** The frozen sign-randomised IBD statistic works on my admissible family-L instance at `τ=0`, and it remains strong under the frozen `τ=2` protocol. The delayed result is not a fair robustness result, however: the comparator is forced to regress on current action even though the plant uses `a_(t−2)`. A one-line, delay-aware feature correction raises comparator AUC to 0.997 in both confounder conditions and eliminates the apparent confounding benefit. The two method specifications also lack a callable path for the raw vectors used by the primary, give the probed comparator two incompatible calibration lifecycles, and prescribe fixtures that contradict the contract's observation-noise equation.

The frozen gate itself passes in the exact required environment: Python 3.12 with NumPy 2.4.4 produced 34/34 passing assertions, 35/35 registered mutants killed, and exit code 0. That result does not cover either new method specification.

## Independent end-to-end implementation

`independent_end_to_end.py` independently implements the draft-3 sign-randomised arm and draft-1 passive comparator on one fixed family-L contract-B instance. The instance has `N_b=4, N_d=2, N_w=4, K=2`, four noise-only padding channels, a complete loss of actuator 0 before step 1,000, and the contract noise scales. The post-event support contains `b2,b3,d1` at `τ=0`, and `b2,b3` at `τ=2`. Minimum retained effects are 0.35 directly and 0.1925 downstream, above `ε=0.05`. The closed-loop radii are 0.588 (`τ=0`) and 0.679 (`τ=2`); action saturation is 4.0%; maximum action–confounded-distractor correlation is 0.818. These satisfy the frozen instance constraints checked here.

Forty scored seeds were used. Values below are means with Monte Carlo standard errors in parentheses. `Δ` is IBD minus passive comparator. “Benefit” is `Δ_present − Δ_absent`, paired by seed.

It is not a clean confirmation of the `δ_AUC=0.10` decision rule: the `N_x=10` benefit is only 0.103, and the equal-weight mean across `N_x={10,30}` is 0.127 with SE 0.015; its normal-approximation 95% lower endpoint is 0.098. The frozen confirmation has 10 seeds before the pilot's permitted increase.

### Choices the specifications left to me

These are all implementation choices I encountered that could affect replication:

1. I used R0 only; the prompt did not specify an R1 transform for this reproduction.
2. I chose and disclosed one fixed admissible instance: matrices, sparsity, gains, observation assignment, four noise-only padding channels, policy feedback, `W_u`, `G`, initial zero state, and actuator-0 loss. The specs do not provide a canonical contract-B fixture or instance seed.
3. I used separate deterministic RNG streams for environment noise and probe signs. Present/absent cells share instance and noise; the `G=0` change causes their state/action paths to diverge, as the contract implies.
4. I fitted a separate comparator for every `(N_x, τ, confounder)` cell on 20 fault-free, non-probed episodes, then scored 40 disjoint seeds. The specs do not say whether a predictor-fit object is shared across the paired present/absent cells.
5. The passive arm received an unprobed task stream; IBD received replaced-action probes. Their exogenous noise keys match, but their endogenous trajectories necessarily diverge.
6. I interpreted `o(t_p)` as the observation immediately **before** applying the probe at step `t_p`, so `h=1` is the first hit at `τ=0`, matching contract C1/C4. Draft 3's pseudocode instead pushes `tr.obs` and indexes `ring[t_p]`, which naturally means the post-action observation. That alternative changes mean IBD AUC from about 0.895–0.900 to 0.839–0.846.
7. At offset 500, IBD uses the last closed epoch (newest anchor 1,480); the comparator uses residual-producing transitions 1,001–1,500. The event is applied before action step 1,000.
8. Per-episode AUC is computed across all observation channels using pairwise probability of superiority, with a half credit for ties. Noise-only padding remains four separate negatives; there are no copies in my instance.
9. I used the comparator's specified float64 Cholesky ridge solve, raw observation/action units, `ddof=1`, clipping order, and right-closed 500-step residual window. The specification does not state a failure policy if Cholesky fails despite the ridge.
10. I did not fit either isotonic map or an alarm threshold because the primary is explicitly the raw-statistic AUC. The still-underdefined secondary and co-primary calibration protocol therefore cannot affect these numbers.
11. The `τ=2` table follows the frozen current-action comparator. I separately tested the obvious delay-aware action feature as a hostile-review sensitivity; it is not substituted into the primary table.
12. One fixed instance is shared across scored seeds. The normative files require fresh instance draws for confirmation but do not define how many instances, their seed hierarchy, or how episode seeds nest within them.

Reproducible row-level output is in `independent_end_to_end_results.csv`; code and summaries are in `independent_end_to_end.py`, `independent_end_to_end_results.json`, `method_sensitivities.py`, and `method_sensitivities.json`.

## New-category statement relative to rounds 1–3

Yes. New categories are:

- **Delay-misaligned comparator features turning a robustness cell into structural misspecification** (R4-CX-01).
- **Cross-channel unit dependence introduced by the trace-scaled ridge penalty** (R4-CX-02).
- **A primary raw vector named in the ledger but absent from the callable estimator interface** (R4-CX-03).
- **Acceptance fixtures made impossible by the contract's additive observation noise** (R4-CX-04).
- **Two incompatible calibration lifecycles inside one comparator specification** (R4-CX-05).
- **Random sign-cell ineligibility being encoded as evidence of absent support** (R4-CX-09).
- **The impossibility of behaviourally enforcing sign blindness when signed action is an allowed input** (R4-CX-10).

Calibration-split ambiguity, primary power, obsolete aggregation coverage and ARL prose contradictions repeat categories seen in earlier rounds, although the concrete failures and mutant are new.
