### Choices the specifications left to me

- I read the perturbation set as the full 3×3 Cartesian product at the defaults N_x=10 and τ=0. I fit the predictor separately for each drawn instance and confounder condition, using episode IDs 100–119; episode IDs 0–3 were scored. Whether fitting is per instance or pooled across instances is not specified (R5-CX-06).
- The probe RNG was a domain-separated `SeedSequence` keyed by logical configuration seed, episode seed, N_x, τ, coupling and noise, shared across present/absent. The specs provide neither this key nor an estimator RNG lifecycle (R5-CX-14).
- I used the generator clock literally: probes at action indices 20,…,1980. The stated terminal grant at t=2000 cannot be supplied to `Instance.run(T=2000)`. At offset 1000 the passive arm uses its last emitted transition score (t=1999); IBD uses the last closed epoch at or before observation time 2000 (R5-CX-12).
- Present/absent runs were fresh deep copies of the same certified draw, with identical episode and probe seeds and only `G[:] = 0` changed. Labels were the post-event `S_obs`; AUC used half credit for ties. No isotonic, operating-point or alarm artefact was fitted because the requested primary uses raw statistics only.

## Attacks that failed

- **Tie-corrected rank-sum equation:** on `plus=[1,2,2]`, `minus=[2,3,3]`, the hand mid-rank/tie calculation and my independent function both gave z = −1.649915822768611 (absolute error 0). The frozen variance formula is correct.
- **H-horizon loading recurrence:** an independent finite-difference rollout of an arbitrary fitted-map-shaped system matched `J^(h)=J^(h−1)β_o+β_a,h−1` through h=3 with maximum error 2.22×10⁻16. The zero-later-action convention is coherent with contract C2/C4, though its fit hierarchy remains unspecified.
- **Balanced-block combinatorics:** over 100,000 consecutive 25-unit windows at K=2, every `(k,sign)` count was 5–7. The `n_min_sign=3` guard cannot bind once the block and clock are implemented as intended. This does not cure the missing RNG lifecycle (R5-CX-14).
- **IBD confounder insensitivity on the frozen runs:** its mean AUC changed only 0.006–0.020 between present and absent at offset 500 across the four base cells. The causal-randomisation intuition survived this bounded test.
- **Runtime and requested replication:** the complete 10×4 base run plus the nine perturbations took 594 seconds; no seeds were dropped, within the requested roughly 15-minute box.
- **Registered gate claim:** under the required interpreter, the stated current criterion genuinely exits 0 and all 40 already-registered mutants are killed. The failed attack is “the reported exit code is false”; R5-CX-01/R5-CX-13 instead attack what that criterion covers.
