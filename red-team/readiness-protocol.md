# Readiness protocol and convergence rule

Date: 6 September 2026. Written after Daniel asked why "ready" was declared five times and disproved five times.

## Why it kept happening

Readiness was measured as "the last reviewer's findings are closed". Each round opened a new defect category (prior art, estimand, mathematics, physics and scope, proofs-were-not-tests), each repair added unreviewed material, and two reviewer patches were applied without independent checking and later reversed. Findings per round did not decline, so extrapolating to zero had no basis.

## What ready means from now on [N]

A frozen version is ready when all of the following are green, none of which is an opinion:
1. Coverage matrix: zero uncovered normative items.
2. Every mutant makes at least one test fail; a reintroduced historical bug fails the gate.
3. Black-box acceptance suite, written by an agent other than the builder, from `interface-spec.md` only, passes.
4. Daniel's hand-computed rectangular, noisy, censored case matches both the pipeline and `contract_ref.py`.
5. Runtime pilot has run and per-cell durations are recorded.
6. Convergence rule below is satisfied.

## Convergence rule for the three reviewers [N]

1. **Freeze.** One version, one hash, no edits during review. Current frozen set: `roadmap-v4.md`, `stage-0a-contract-v3.md`, `interface-spec.md`, `confirmation-design.csv`, `executable-proofs/gate/`. Hash: `c197652c0d8e846b`.
2. **Parallel review.** Claude, Codex and Gemini review the same hash with the same rubric: ID, severity (high, medium, low), claim attacked, evidence with sources, proposed fix, fix type (test, prose, decision).
3. **Adjudication.** Each reviewer rates the other two reviewers' findings: valid, invalid, or judgment call, with one line of reason. Applied: findings with two-of-three validity or with executable evidence. Parked with dissent recorded: one-vote findings. Sent to Daniel: judgment calls.
4. **Judgment calls are Daniel's.** Not consensus items. Current list: Phase S publication, the consciousness sentence, package name, venue.
5. **Stopping rule.** Two consecutive frozen versions with no high-severity finding from any reviewer and no new finding category. Then build.
6. **Cap.** At most two further prose rounds. After the cap, remaining risk is in code and the gate is the reviewer.
