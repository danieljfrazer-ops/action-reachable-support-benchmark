**Version 3, 7 September 2026.** Supersedes v2 (archived at `superseded-specs/comparator-spec.v2.md`), which supersedes v1 (`review4-claude-opus/comparator-spec.v1.md`). Basis: `stage-0a-contract-v3.9.md` §§0, A0, B, C, E, F, G, H, H2, L; `interface-spec-v5.md`; `decisions-required.md` D-11 (all six parts adopted); `round5-adjudication-and-tally.md`; `executable-proofs/gate/reference_generator.py` and `contract_ref.py` as frozen at contract v3.9. **What v3 changes.** One thing, and everything that follows from it. **D-11.2(a): the innovation-mean-shift term of v2 §3 is deleted.** Round 5 proved, three implementations of three, that it cannot see an actuator loss under a centred policy — the loss changes the innovation's *covariance with the action*, not its mean — and that subtracting it cost 0.12 to 0.27 AUC, which is why the D-10.3 competence floor failed (§15). It is replaced by the **action-residual covariance score** Δ_c (§3), which is the object the loss actually moves. Consequent changes: the primary statistic becomes `raw_support_c = −Δ_c` and the H-horizon loading survives **only** in the secondary full-channel statistic, which is the loading itself, `secondary_support_c = l_c`, frozen at `fit_predictor` (D-11.2a as amended in contract v3.9 §H(2), §4.2; the difference `l_c − Δ_c` was considered for the secondary and rejected before the freeze, §16 C-2); the primary AUC is now computed over the pre-event support (D-11.1a, §4.4); every learned artefact is fitted **per instance** with a declared fit-split episode-seed registry (D-11.6, §1.1); the alarm clock runs from reset with `persistence_unit = "steps"` and `count_alarms_timed` (D-11.5, §6); and the offset read convention for a step arm is fixed (§4.5). The predictor (§1), the loading (§2), the degeneracy rule (§5), the probed variant (§7) and the calibration tier (§8) are v2's, with the round-5 defects dispositioned in §15.

**Provisional constants introduced by v3 (stated here as the contract requires).** Two new constants are not inherited from contract §0 and are marked **[prov]** in §11: **Δ_cap = 1e4** (the cap on Δ_c, a numerical guard) and **κ_min = 1e-4** (the relative eigenvalue cutoff for the action-covariance whitener). Three v2 provisionals survive unchanged (`σ_deg_rel`, `l_deg`, the CUSUM `k` multiplier). Three v2 provisionals are **promoted to frozen with a stated derivation** (`λ_rel`, `n_pred_fit`, `n_warm`), which is R5-21's remedy. One new constant, **λ_a = 1e-6** (the ridge on the action covariance), is frozen rather than provisional, for the reason given in §11 row 19. No constant in this document may be re-valued after the pilot without re-running ARL_0 calibration, the isotonic fit and the black-box acceptance suite (contract E6). **There is no outcome-dependent tuning clause.**

**Notation.** Standardised innovations are **r̃** (never `z`; `z_t` is bound to the latent vector by contract §B). `C` = observation channels, `K` = actuators, `ℋ = {1,2,3}`, `τ_max = 2`, `q` = the retained rank of the action-covariance whitener (§3.2), `n` = the trailing-window length, `t_ep` = the episode step index. Sections are **[N]** normative or **[I]** informative.

**Diagnostic numbers in this document.** Every number quoted from execution was produced by `superseded-specs/comparator-v3-diagnostic.py` (output alongside it) on **one configuration seed, one episode seed, one implementation, no intervals**. Each is labelled *single-instance diagnostic*. **None is a result**, none is an interval, and none discharges the D-10.3 competence floor, which is a per-cell interval-bounded quantity that round 6 must execute.

## 1. Training population and the delay-aware predictor [N]

### 1.1 Fit hierarchy, per instance (D-11.6; closes R5-CX-06, R5-20)

`fit_predictor` and `fit_alarm_reference` are called **once per (cell, instance_seed, arm)** with data drawn from **that instance only**. Nothing — β, μ, σ, l, the standardisation, the action covariance, (ā, v, k), the calibrator g, the operating point θ*, the threshold h — is pooled across instance draws, across confounder conditions, or across arms except where §7 requires arm 2's and arm 3's predictor artefacts to be bitwise identical. The ledger records the key `(cell, instance_seed, arm)` and the fit-split episode seeds on every artefact (interface v5).

This is not a formality: instances inside one cell are independent SCM draws with different `A_b`, `B`, `C_d`, so a shared β would fit one linear model to ten different plants. v2's "per cell" wording (whose key omits `seed`, unlike `contract_ref.CELL_KEY`) left this open and all three round-5 implementers had to guess; two guessed per instance and one used different episode ids.

**Episode-seed registry [N].** Every population below is generated on the **certified instance** by `Instance.run(2000, ep=<id>)`. Ids are frozen:

| Population | Episode ids | Count | Event? | Probes? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Scored episodes (primary, secondary, P2) | 0–3 | 4 | event-carrying, `event_t = 1000` | arm 3 only |
| Predictor fit split (`fit_predictor`) | 900–919 | 20 | **never** | never (both arms) |
| Alarm reference, arm 2 (`fit_alarm_reference`) | 920–939 | 20 | never | never |
| Alarm reference, arm 3 (`fit_alarm_reference`) | 940–959 | 20 | never | **yes**, the seq-IBD stream |
| Calibrator fit split (harness) | 2000–2023 | 24 | 12 of 24 | per arm |
| Operating-point validation split (harness) | 2100–2115 | 16 | 8 of 16 | per arm |
| ARL_0 calibration streams (harness, D-2a) | 3000–3399 | ≤ 400 | **never** | per arm |

All ids are pairwise disjoint, disjoint from the scored episodes, and disjoint from the generator's oracle-only `RESERVED_EP = {996, 997, 998, 999}`. Every id is below 100000, so no main stream collides with another episode's burn-in prefix, which the generator keys at `ep + 100000`. An implementation that uses other ids is non-conforming; three implementations that use different ids do not agree to Monte Carlo error, which is the round-6 exit condition (contract A0).

**Fault-free means fault-free [N] (closes R5-22).** `Instance.apply_event` mutates `B`, `avail` and `gain` **in place with no undo**, and `Instance.run(..., event_t=...)` calls it mid-rollout. Every population in the table above is therefore generated on a **fresh `copy.deepcopy` of the certified instance**, and an event-carrying episode is run on its own deep copy. Reusing one instance object across episodes silently runs episodes 2..4 with the actuator already dead from `t = 0`, which is not a fault-free fit split, not a fresh-start ARL clock and not a pre-event segment. **T-CMP-freshcopy.**

**Confounder-absent construction [N] (closes R5-13).** The absent arm is the **same instance draw and the same noise streams** with `G = 0` and `W_u` unchanged (contract §G), obtained by `absent = copy.deepcopy(inst); absent.G[:] = 0.0` on the **already-certified** present instance. It is **not** re-certified and **not** re-drawn: `draw_certified({"G_scale": 0}, seed)` raises on the frozen generator, because with `G = 0` the ρ_min witness collapses (`rho_witness ≈ 0.055–0.081` against `rho_min = 0.4`) and every sub-seed is rejected. v2 §2 cited that call as its headline diagnostic; the citation was not executable and is withdrawn. The absent arm gets **its own** `fit_predictor` and `fit_alarm_reference` calls on episodes 900–919 / 920–939 **of the absent stream**; no artefact is ever shared between the present and absent arms. Present/absent differences are within-instance contrasts.

### 1.2 Population and design

**Population.** `n_pred_fit = 20` whole fault-free, in-distribution, **non-probed** episodes (ids 900–919) of the instance being scored: 40,000 transitions per (instance, confounder condition). Delivered through `fit_predictor(transitions)` (interface v5).

**Design.** Feature row for the transition `(prev_obs = o_t, applied_action = a_t, obs = o_{t+1})`:

  **φ_t = [ o_t (C), a_t (K), a_{t−1} (K), a_{t−2} (K), 1 ] ∈ R^{C+3K+1}**

Lag depth is `τ_max = 2` from the contract registry, never the cell's `τ`: the arm uses the same feature layout in every cell and never reads the true delay. Target `Y_t = o_{t+1}`. One shared coefficient matrix `β ∈ R^{(C+3K+1)×C}` — channel-agnostic by construction.

**Lag handling at episode start.** A two-slot action buffer is initialised to `0 ∈ R^K` at every episode boundary: at step index 0 both lag slots are zero, at index 1 the `a_{t−2}` slot is zero. This is exact, not imputed: `Instance.run` re-initialises its delay queue to `[zeros(K)] * τ` **after** the burn-in prefix, so the plant's own pre-episode action history is zeros. No transition is dropped for it; transitions are never formed across a boundary. The arm detects a boundary from the transition's `t` field (`t = 0`, or `t ≠ t_prev + 1`); interface v5 has no `reset` call. **T-CMP-lagstart.**

**Standardisation of penalised features.** On the fit split, for each of the `C + 3K` non-intercept columns: `m_j = mean(φ_{·,j})`, `s_j = max(sd(φ_{·,j}, ddof=0), s_floor)`, `s_floor = 1e-8`. The standardised design is `X̃ = [(φ − m)/s , 1]`. The intercept is neither standardised nor penalised. Each penalised column then has `‖x̃_j‖² = n` exactly. **The action block of this same standardisation — `m_a = m[C:C+3K]`, `s_a = s[C:C+3K]` — is the standardisation §3 uses for ã. There is exactly one standardisation in this arm.**

**Ridge.** `β̃ = (X̃ᵀX̃ + λ D)^{-1} X̃ᵀY`, `D = diag(1,…,1,0)`, float64 Cholesky, and

  **λ = λ_rel · (1/(C+3K)) Σ_j ‖x̃_j‖² = λ_rel · n**, with **λ_rel = 1e-4** (frozen; §11 row 8).

Because the penalty acts on columns of equal norm, λ and every fitted loading are invariant to rescaling any observation channel or actuator (R4-CX-02). **T-CMP-scale.**

**Back-transform.** Scoring uses raw features, so the coefficients are transformed back once at fit time: `β_raw[j,:] = β̃[j,:] / s_j` for `j = 1..C+3K`; `b_raw = β̃_intercept − Σ_j (m_j / s_j) β̃[j,:]`. `X̃β̃ ≡ φ β_raw + b_raw` identically; asserted to 1e-10 in **T-CMP-backtransform**.

**Innovations.** `R = Y − X̃β̃` on the fit split; `μ_c = mean(R_{·,c})`, `σ_c = max(sd(R_{·,c}, ddof=1), σ_floor)`, `σ_floor = 1e-6`. Online `r̃_{t,c} = clip((r_{t,c} − μ_c)/σ_c, ±z_cap)`, `z_cap = 8`.

**Probe hygiene.** Any transition in a `fit_predictor` population carrying `probe_flag = True` is dropped **with the following `τ_max = 2`** — a probe at `t` contaminates `b` through `t+1+τ`. The confirmatory fit population is non-probed for both arms, so this is a guard (F9).

**Frozen online.** `β, μ, σ, l, degenerate, m_a, s_a, W_a, q` are computed once in `fit_predictor` and never updated during scoring: no online refit, no forgetting factor, no recursive least squares. The arm is deliberately a **frozen residual monitor** — the class of method the working claim is about.

**No randomness.** The arm draws no random numbers at any point. Its outputs are a deterministic function of the transition stream and the frozen artefacts, so the estimator-RNG lifecycle that round 5 found undefined (R5-CX-14) does not arise for this arm. Arm 3's probe stream is externally injected (§7).

## 2. H-horizon action loading, secondary only [N] (D-10.2, restricted by D-11.2a)

Write the raw-unit blocks of `β_raw` as `β_o ∈ R^{C×C}` (the `o_t` block) and `β_{a,0}, β_{a,1}, β_{a,2} ∈ R^{K×C}`. Define the **iterated-prediction sensitivity** by rolling the fitted one-step map forward with the intercept and the noise dropped:

  **J^{(0)} = 0 ∈ R^{K×C};  J^{(h)} = J^{(h−1)} β_o + β_{a,h−1},  h = 1, 2, 3.**

  **l_c = max_{h ∈ ℋ} ‖ J^{(h)}_{:,c} ‖₂ / σ_c**,  ℋ = {1,2,3}. Frozen at `fit_predictor`.

**What J^{(h)} is, labelled (closes R5-GM-02).** `J^{(h)}` is an **open-loop empirical sensitivity of the fitted one-step predictor**: the iterate treats actions after the first step as exactly zero, mirroring contract C2/C4's `do(a_t = a, later actions 0)`. It is **not** a closed-loop sensitivity — the default policy does respond at every future step, and that response is deliberately not modelled — and it is **not** an estimate of the causal Jacobian `M` (C4). Both facts are restated in §14. The recursion itself is exact under its stated convention; two round-5 reviewers tried to break it and failed.

**Where l is now used [N].** `l` **is** the secondary full-channel statistic (§4.2), in full and unmodified. It does **not** enter the primary. Round 5 established that on the old full-channel primary the score `q = l − shift` was strictly dominated by `l` alone (R5-3: 0.875/0.808/0.876/0.807 against 0.994/0.987/0.997/0.993 absent). Under D-11.1a the primary is restricted to the pre-event support, where **every** channel was action-reachable before the event, so `l` separates positives from negatives only through the incidental size of their loadings — it carries no evidence about which of them the event removed, and on the frozen family it carries evidence in the **wrong** direction (§4.4). Removing it from the primary is therefore not a tuning choice; it is what the re-posed estimand requires.

**Properties, all testable and retained from v2.** A downstream `d` channel has `J^{(1)} = 0` but gains loading at `h = 2` through the fitted `b → d` entries of `β_o` (retained `d` on the pinned instance: `l = 12.8` against 0.017 for the one-lag arm). At `τ = 2` the action reaches `b` only at `h = 3`, carried by `β_{a,2}` (retained body channels 7.8 / 6.4 against 0.056 / 0.233). Rescaling channel `c′` by γ acts on `β_o` as a similarity transform and scales `σ_c` with the numerator, so `l_c` is unchanged (**T-CMP-scale**). **The confounding pathology is preserved deliberately:** `a_t` is a contemporaneous proxy for `u_t`, which drives `x_{t+1}`, so confounded distractors keep a large loading — *single-instance diagnostic*: `l ≈ 6.8` on confounded distractors against `l ≈ 0.04–0.14` on unconfounded ones and 3.0–9.8 on the support. v3 makes the arm competent where competence is possible and leaves the shared-cause failure where the benchmark measures it.

## 3. Action-residual covariance change statistic Δ_c [N] (D-11.2a)

### 3.1 Definition

Let `ã_i ∈ R^{3K}` be the lagged-action features of transition `i`, standardised with the **fit-split** mean and scale of §1.2:

  **ã_i = ( [a_i, a_{i−1}, a_{i−2}] − m_a ) / s_a**

(the lag slots use the same zero-fill at an episode boundary as the design). Let `r̃_{i,c}` be the standardised innovations of §1.2. Over the trailing window of `n = min(W, t_ep)` steps ending at the current transition,

  **ĉ_c = (1/n) Σ_{i=t−n+1}^{t} r̃_{i,c} ã_i ∈ R^{3K}**,  **Δ_c(t) = sqrt( n · ĉ_cᵀ Σ̂_a^{+} ĉ_c ) ≥ 0**

with `Σ̂_a` the fit-split covariance of `ã` and `Σ̂_a^{+}` its regularised inverse as §3.2 defines it. Δ_c is a score-type statistic for the hypothesis "the innovation of channel c is uncorrelated with the recent action history", evaluated on the trailing window against the fit-split action geometry. It is **non-negative and sign-blind by construction**, which is what a change statistic must be: the arm is not told whether an actuator was added or removed.

### 3.2 Regularisation and rank handling [N]

Because `ã` is standardised by the fit-split mean and scale, the fit-split second moment is exactly the fit-split covariance and has **exact unit diagonal**. Define, on the fit split of `n_fit` retained rows,

  **Σ̂_a = (1/n_fit) Ãᵀ Ã + λ_a I**,  **λ_a = 1e-6** (frozen).

Take the symmetric eigendecomposition `Σ̂_a = V diag(ν) Vᵀ` (`numpy.linalg.eigh` or an equivalent symmetric solver; eigenvalues ascending). **Retain** the directions with

  **ν_i ≥ κ_min · max_j ν_j**,  **κ_min = 1e-4 [prov]**,

let `q` be the number retained, and freeze the `q × 3K` **whitener**

  **W_a = diag(ν_q^{−1/2}) V_qᵀ**,  **ǎ_i = W_a ã_i ∈ R^q**.

Then `Δ_c(t) = √n · ‖ (1/n) Σ_i r̃_{i,c} ǎ_i ‖₂` exactly, which is the form the online recursion computes (§13) and is identical to the quadratic form of §3.1 with `Σ̂_a^{+}` the eigen-truncated inverse. `q` and `cond(Σ̂_a) = max ν / min ν` are logged in the ledger as fit artefacts.

**Why the cutoff is relative to the largest eigenvalue and not to λ_a.** The ridge alone does not handle collinearity: an exactly collinear lag pair leaves a null direction whose ridged eigenvalue is exactly λ_a = 1e-6, and whitening it multiplies that direction's noise by `1/√λ_a = 1000`, so a rank-deficient Σ̂_a would produce an enormous Δ from pure noise. *Single-instance diagnostic* (`comparator-v3-diagnostic.py`, synthetic 6-column design with one duplicated column): at `κ_min = 1e-8` the cutoff is 2e-8, the null direction is retained and the largest whitening factor is **1000**; at `κ_min = 1e-4` the cutoff is 2e-4, the null direction is dropped (`q = 5`) and the largest whitening factor is **1.028**. On the frozen family nothing is truncated: `q = 3K = 6` with `cond(Σ̂_a) = 213` at `τ = 0` and `274` at `τ = 2`, so the smallest retained eigenvalue sits about 37× above the cutoff. λ_a is a conditioning guard on a unit-diagonal matrix and has no lever on the statistic (§11 row 19). **T-CMP-whiten**, **T-CMP-rank**.

### 3.3 Null and alternative behaviour [N, one paragraph as D-11.2a requires]

Under the null — fault-free, in-distribution, the frozen predictor still correct — the innovations are uncorrelated with the action history, so `√n ĉ_c` is approximately a mean-zero `R^{3K}` vector whose whitened covariance is the identity (the standardisation of r̃ by σ_c supplies the unit scale), `Δ_c²` is approximately `χ²_q` with `q = 3K` generically, and **Δ_c is O(√(3K))** — 2.449 at K = 2. *Single-instance diagnostic*: over a whole fault-free episode past warm-up, the median Δ over channels is 2.55 (τ = 0) and 2.48 (τ = 2) with maxima 6.19 and 5.07, against the predicted 2.449; the fault-free values on supported and unsupported channels are indistinguishable, which is exactly what a change statistic should do before a change. After a **complete loss of actuator k** the frozen predictor keeps adding `β_{a,τ,k,c}·a_{i−τ,k}` to a plant that no longer responds, so the innovation acquires the deterministic component `−β_{a,τ,k,c}·a_{i−τ,k}/σ_c`, the corresponding entry of `ĉ_c` converges to `−β_{a,τ,k,c}·sd(a_k)/σ_c`, and **Δ_c grows as √n · |β_{a,k,c}| · sd(a_k) / σ_c** — linearly in the square root of the window, so the statistic separates further from the null the longer the post-event window runs, which is the behaviour the offsets {200, 500, 1000} are there to exhibit. **Which lag slot carries the signal:** the one the plant actually uses — at **τ = 0** the `(lag 0, actuator k)` slot, at **τ = 2** the `(lag 2, actuator k)` slot (*single-instance diagnostic*, post-event window t = 1000..1499, un-whitened ĉ on the lost channel: `[−7.30, −5.38, −6.25, −4.66, −5.00, −3.76]` at τ = 0, largest in slot `(a_t, k=0)`; `[−4.53, −3.46, −5.55, −4.40, −6.67, −5.33]` at τ = 2, largest in slot `(a_{t−2}, k=0)`) — and the neighbouring slots load too, because the closed-loop action is autocorrelated, which is precisely why `Σ̂_a^{+}` is in the formula: without it the same evidence would be counted once per lag and the arm would need to know τ to know where to look, which its information set forbids.

### 3.4 What Δ_c does not see [N, declared failure modes]

- **It fires on retained channels too.** Δ_c measures how much of channel `c`'s predicted drive came from the lost actuator, not whether `c` left the support. A channel that keeps its support through another actuator but drew heavily on the lost one also gets a large Δ. *Single-instance diagnostic*, offset 500: the lost channel 270.8, retained body channels 174.2 / 176.2 / 126.6, retained downstream 8.6 / 19.9, null channels ≈ 2–4. The primary ranking works through the **magnitude ordering**, and it works on this family because CL-4 is certified by construction — the lost channel is the dominant body component of the event actuator, driven by no other actuator and fed by no other body component (contract A0), so it is the channel whose drive was lost *entirely*. A channel that loses a large fraction of a large drive can outrank a channel that loses all of a small one. This is a property of the statistic, declared in advance, not a defect to be repaired after seeing round-6 outcomes.
- **Downstream losses are seen weakly.** For a `d` channel, `d_{t+1} = A_d d_t + C_d b_t` and `b_t` is observed, so a one-step predictor needs almost no action coefficient; what action loading `d` has comes from errors-in-variables on the noisy `b` observation. Δ therefore moves on `d` channels after the event but by an order of magnitude less than on `b` channels (*single-instance diagnostic*: 2.6 → 8.6 and 1.8 → 19.9 against 3.5 → 270.8). **If a confirmatory event ever removes only downstream channels, this arm is expected to be near chance on the primary.** On the frozen family it does not: `n_lost = 1` and the lost channel is a body channel.
- **It is not a level-α test.** The innovations are neither independent nor Gaussian under the closed-loop policy, and the χ² reference is asymptotic and unused: Δ is a ranking statistic and an alarm input, never a p-value. No size claim is made (§14).
- **The window mean of ã is not re-centred.** ã is centred by the fit-split mean, not by the window mean, so a window in which the action mean drifts contributes a second-order `mean(r̃)·mean(ã)` term. Under the null `mean(r̃) ≈ 0` and the term is negligible; it is stated because it is a real approximation, and because arm 3's probe blocks are the one place it could bite (§7).

## 4. The two returned statistics [N] (D-11.2a, D-11.1a)

### 4.1 Primary statistic (returned by `update`)

  **Δ̂_c(t) = min(Δ_c(t), Δ_cap)**, `Δ_cap = 1e4` **[prov]**
  **raw_support_c(t) = −Δ̂_c(t)** ∈ [−Δ_cap, 0], **higher = still controlled**
  degenerate channels (§5): **raw_support_c ≡ raw_absent = −(Δ_cap + 1) = −10001**, strictly below every live value.

### 4.2 Secondary full-channel statistic (returned by `secondary_support()`)

  **secondary_support_c(t) = l_c** ∈ [0, max_c l_c], **higher = in S^obs,ε**, **constant in `t`**, frozen at `fit_predictor`
  degenerate channels: **secondary_support_c ≡ l_absent = −1**.

The loading is the secondary statistic in full: **unclipped, unmodified and independent of the scored episode** (contract v3.9 §H(2)). It needs no cap — `l_c ≥ 0` by construction and its largest value on the frozen family is ≈ 12.8 — so `Δ_cap` does not touch it, and `l_absent = −1` is strictly below every live value. v2's separate `q_cap = 40` is retired, and so is v3's shared sentinel name `q_absent`: the primary's `raw_absent = −(Δ_cap + 1) = −10001` and the secondary's `l_absent = −1` are each strictly below their own statistic's live range, which is all a sentinel has to do (R5-15).

**What the secondary reports [N].** `secondary_support = l` is this arm's **static-structure competence**: the ranking, over all `C` channels, that the frozen predictor's H-horizon action loading gives to the plant's action-reachable structure. It is scored by `contract_ref.auc_prob_superiority` over all `C` channels against `S^obs,ε(post)` (§4.4). **It never sees the event** — it is fitted on the event-free episodes 900–919 and does not change during the scored episode — so what it reports is how well a passive frozen linear model recovers reachable structure, and nothing else. Three consequences are declared in advance rather than repaired. Confounded distractors keep their **large** loading (§2: `l ≈ 6.8` against 3.0–9.8 on the support), because `a_t` is a contemporaneous proxy for `u_t`; that false support is the shared-cause failure the benchmark exists to measure. Unconfounded distractors and pads rank low (`l ≈ 0.04–0.14`), which is the competence half. And after the event the lost channel keeps its **stale** pre-event loading and is therefore still ranked as supported, which costs the secondary a little at every post-event offset — the price of a statistic that does not track change, and the reason the change-tracking is done by the primary and only by the primary.

**Δ_c serves the primary alone**, hence one window, one warm-up, one cap and one degeneracy rule. There is no second window, no second cadence and no second clipping regime anywhere in this arm; the secondary adds none, because it is a frozen vector.

- **Window.** Trailing, right-closed, `[t−n+1, t]` in transition index, `n = min(W, t_ep)`, `W = 500`, per episode, never reset mid-episode, never carried across an episode boundary.
- **Warm-up.** For `t_ep < n_warm = max(30, 5·3K) = 30` (K = 2), **Δ_c := 0 for every channel**, so `raw_support ≡ 0` (a constant vector, mid-rank AUC exactly 0.5); `secondary_support ≡ l` at every step, warm-up or not. The warm-up applies to **Δ only** and never to the alarm channel (§6). It cannot bind at any scored offset (`t_ep ≥ 1200`).
- **Cadence.** Recomputed **every environment step**; a probe step is an environment step. `update` returns `(raw_support, p, stat, raise)` every step and `secondary_support()` is read by the harness after each `update` (interface v5). `p = g(secondary_support)` — see §8; because the secondary is constant in `t`, so is `p`. `persistence_unit = "steps"`; no epoch quantisation.
- **Determinism of the accumulation.** The normative definition of `ĉ_c` is the sum over the window in increasing `i`. An implementation may maintain the incremental accumulator of §13 (add the entering outer product, subtract the leaving one) instead of re-summing; **T-CMP-window-drift** asserts the two agree to 1e-10 relative over a 2,000-step episode.
- **Tie rounding.** Both statistics are rounded to **12 significant decimal digits** (`float(f"{v:.11e}")`) before return — `raw_support` at every step, `secondary_support` once at `fit_predictor`, since it never changes — so that ties which are structural (warm-up zeros, degenerate sentinels, channels of equal loading) are exact ties in every implementation and ties which are numerical accidents are not created. Round 5 needed a tie convention and none existed; this is it.

### 4.3 Orientation and clipping, restated as an invariant

Both statistics point the same way (higher = more support / more still-controlled) and both are bounded. `secondary_support = l` is bounded by construction, is not clipped, and passes through no cap; only the primary meets `Δ_cap`. The cap is a **numerical guard**: on the frozen family the largest Δ at any step of a post-event episode is ≈ 273 (*single-instance diagnostic*), 37× below Δ_cap, and the fraction of scored (channel, offset) pairs at the cap is reported by the pilot. **Any nonzero fraction is a defect to investigate, not a result** — the same standing this document gives the degeneracy rule (§5). This is R5-15's remedy: the guards are declared unreachable in advance rather than presented as live machinery.

### 4.4 What the primary AUC asks of this statistic [N] (D-11.1a)

The **primary** is `contract_ref.auc_pre_event_support(raw_support, S^obs,ε(pre), S^obs,ε(post))` at each offset in `offsets_rank`, primary 500: the mid-rank AUC restricted to channels in the pre-event support, with **positives = channels still in S^obs,ε after the event** and **negatives = the lost set**. So the arm's primary statistic must be **larger on channels the event left controlled than on channels the event removed** — that is, Δ must be **larger on the lost set**. Channels the actions never controlled (distractors, world, padding) never enter; the ranking is invariant to any monotone calibrator, so the primary uses no supervised data.

Contract §G requires the **fit-time-constant control** to score 0.5 ± Monte Carlo. **This arm cannot**, in either direction, and that is the point of D-11.1a: `−Δ_c` is computed from the scored episode's trailing window, which after the event is made of post-event transitions, so it is not a fit-time artefact and its ranking responds to the event. Its expected primary AUC exceeds 0.5 exactly to the extent that Δ is larger on lost channels than on retained ones — no more, and by §3.4 not always. *Single-instance diagnostic*: `AUC_pre(−Δ) = 1.000` at all three offsets, present and absent, at τ = 0 and τ = 2 on seed 0. **That is one instance with one negative channel, where the AUC can only take the six values {0, 0.2, …, 1.0}; it is evidence that the statistic is computable and oriented correctly, and nothing else.**

**A related observation, reported not repaired.** Contract §G states that "a fit-time constant, or any statistic that never sees the scored episode, scores exactly 0.5". That is exact for a statistic constant **across channels**; it is not true of a fit-time-constant **vector** such as `l`, whose ranking among pre-event-support channels is fixed but not neutral. On the frozen family it is systematically *worse* than chance, because CL-4-by-construction makes the lost channel the dominant body component of the event actuator, which has near-maximal loading. *Single-instance diagnostic*: the static `l` control scores **0.200** (τ = 0, present and absent) and **0.000** (τ = 2) on the primary. That vector is also this arm's secondary statistic (§4.2), but the contract's control statement is about the **primary**, which is where it fails to hold. This document does not change the contract; §16 records the observation (C-1).

**The secondary full-channel AUC** is `contract_ref.auc_prob_superiority(secondary_support, S^obs,ε(post))` over all `C` channels, co-reported with AUPRC, prevalence and the grouped AUPRC. It is where the loading term and the static-structure competence are reported, and contract §G attaches **no floor** to it. Because `secondary_support = l` is frozen at `fit_predictor` and **never sees the event**, this is a pre-event quantity scored against a post-event label set: confounded distractors keep their large loading and enter it as false support (§2), unconfounded distractors and pads enter as true negatives, and the lost channel keeps its stale loading and is ranked as supported when it is not — the little the secondary loses after the event. *Single-instance diagnostic* at offset 500: `l` scores **0.800** (present) and **0.958** (absent).

**The rejected variant, retained as the record.** The difference `l_c − Δ̂_c` was considered for the secondary and rejected before the freeze (contract v3.9 §H(2)). Δ after the event is one to two orders of magnitude larger than `l` on every action-coupled channel, so the difference ranks the true support *below* the null distractors: *single-instance diagnostic* at offset 500, secondary AUC **0.063** (present) and **0.189** (absent), against the 0.800 / 0.958 above. Any rescaling that rescued it would have had to be chosen against the outcome, which this spec forbids. The diagnostic (`superseded-specs/comparator-v3-diagnostic.py` and its output) is kept as the record of why the contract's secondary is the loading alone; §16 C-2 records the exchange.

### 4.5 Offset read convention for a step arm [N] (closes R5-CX-12, R5-26)

`Instance.run(T = 2000)` iterates `t = 0 .. 1999`; the transition at step `t` is `(prev_obs = o_t, applied_action = a_t, obs = o_{t+1})` and carries `t` in its `t` field; the event is applied **before** the action at `t = event_t` is taken, so `t = 1000` is the first post-event transition. The arm emits once per transition, so its emission times are `t = 0 .. 1999`.

**Rule (contract v3.9 §G, "Offset read rule, one for every arm", identical in words for both arms): the statistic scored at offset Δ is the arm's output as of its last emission whose transition index `t ≤ event_t + Δ − 1`** — the last `update` call that can precede step `event_t + Δ`. For a step arm that is the emission with `t = event_t + Δ − 1`:

| Offset Δ | 200 | 500 | 1000 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Emission read (this arm, step cadence) | **t = 1199** | **t = 1499** | **t = 1999** |
| Window covered, transition index | [700, 1199] | [1000, 1499] | [1500, 1999] |
| Post-event fraction of the window | **0.400** | **1.000** | **1.000** |
| IBD arm, same rule (epoch grid t ≡ 2 mod 20) | 1182 | 1482 | 1982 |

**Offset 1000 lands on the arm's last transition**: `event_t + 1000 − 1 = 1999 = T − 1`. `Instance.run` iterates `t = 0 .. 1999` and there is **no transition at `t = 2000 = episode_len`**, which is precisely why the contract states the rule at `t ≤ event_t + Δ − 1` rather than at `event_t + Δ`; under this rule offset 1000 needs no special case. The two arms read one rule at different cadences and therefore land on different steps — 1999 against 1982 at offset 1000, and 1199 against 1182 at offset 200, a **17-step gap** that is exactly the coarseness of the IBD arm's 20-step epoch grid; that is a declared property of cadence, not an unspecified convention, and the window mixtures match the IBD arm's to two decimals (0.40 / 1.00 / 1.00). **Offset 200 is a transition-window outcome** for this arm as for the IBD arm (0.600 pre-event, 0.400 post-event) and is reported with those fractions, never as a clean post-event outcome (D-10.5). **T-CMP-offsets.**

## 5. Degenerate channels [N]

Contract §H2 adds `ε^o` to **every** channel including unavailable and padding channels, so **there are no hard-silent channels**. Degeneracy is defined jointly on innovation scale and action loading, both relative and unit-free. Channel `c` is **degenerate** iff

  **σ_c < σ_deg_rel · median_{c′}(σ_{c′})**  **and**  **l_c < l_deg**,  `σ_deg_rel = 1e-2` **[prov]**, `l_deg = 1e-3` **[prov]**.

- Determined **once, at `fit_predictor`**, from the fault-free fit split; never re-evaluated online, so a mid-episode dropout is a live channel whose Δ rises and whose `raw_support` falls — the behaviour the benchmark wants — not a channel that silently leaves the scoring set.
- A degenerate channel gets `raw_support ≡ raw_absent = −(Δ_cap + 1)` and `secondary_support ≡ l_absent = −1` at every step, each strictly below its own statistic's live range, and is **excluded from `𝒞*`**, the set over which the alarm maximum is taken (without the exclusion a channel with σ at the floor would produce an unbounded r̃ and a permanent alarm).
- **Padding channels are not degenerate.** *Single-instance diagnostic*: pads have `σ ≈ 0.0499–0.0502` against a median `σ ≈ 0.1198` (ratio 0.42, against a threshold of 0.01) and `l ≈ 0.037–0.108` (against a threshold of 1e-3). They are ordinary negatives in the secondary, and they stay in `𝒞*`.
- **Declared unreachable (R5-15).** On the frozen family and its perturbation set the rule cannot fire: the smallest σ is 20–40× above its threshold at every point tested. It is a numerical guard for hand-built fixtures and for morphology events. **If it fires on a generator instance in the pilot that is a defect to investigate, not a result.** The sentinel is likewise not a live isotonic knot on this family; v2's claim that it is "a genuine separate knot" is withdrawn (§15).

## 6. Alarm channel and the D-11.5 clock [N]

- **Recursion.** `S_0 = 0`; `m_t = max_{c ∈ 𝒞*} |r̃_{t,c}|`; `S_t = max(0, S_{t−1} + m_t − k)`. `S ← 0` at every episode boundary (the calibrated quantity is the fresh-start run length).
- **Reference value.** `fit_alarm_reference(transitions)` consumes the **arm-specific** fault-free stream (episodes 920–939 non-probed for arm 2, 940–959 probed for arm 3) and sets `ā = mean(m)`, `v = sd(m, ddof=1)`, **`k = ā + 0.5·v`** **[prov]**. It may change **only** `(ā, v, k)`; `β, μ, σ, l, degenerate, m_a, s_a, W_a` are asserted bitwise unchanged across the call (**T-CMP-lifecycle**).
- **Outputs.** `stat = S_t` every step; **`raise = 1 iff S_t > h`, with no persistence applied by the arm**. `statistic_is_monotone_in_alarm = True`.
- **Clock from reset, no prefix (D-11.5; closes R5-CX-09, R5-14 for this arm).** `persistence_unit = "steps"`. The harness records each `update`'s `raise` with its `t` and applies persistence `p = 3` in emissions and refractory `r = 20` in environment steps through `contract_ref.count_alarms_timed(raises, times, p, r)`; because this arm emits at every step from `t = 0`, `times = 0,1,2,…` and `count_alarms_timed` reduces exactly to its step-cadence special case `count_alarms(raw, p, r)` (**T-CMP-timed**). The fresh-start run length is `contract_ref.run_length_from_reset` measured from `t = 0`.
- **ARL_0 protocol consequence for a step arm [N].** This arm declares **no warm-up prefix on the alarm channel**: `S_t` is defined and can exceed `h` from the first transition, and §4's `n_warm` guard applies to Δ only. So the D-11.5 charge — "an arm that needs a warm-up prefix is charged that prefix; the prefix is not excluded from the clock" — costs this arm **nothing**, and the D-2a calibration streams for it need only cover the prefix-free run-length distribution (≈ 3 × ARL_0, plus the censoring the cap implies). The sequential IBD arm pays its 502-step prefix out of the same 2 × 10⁶ cap. **That asymmetry is real, is the intended consequence of D-11.5, and is reported, not repaired**: under one rule the two arms have different dispersion tolerance (round 5 computed sd ≤ 1861 for the prefixed arm against sd ≤ 2282 for this one). Two harness-side dependencies follow and belong to the D-2a protocol, not to this spec: the number of run lengths must satisfy `n ≥ (1.96·ŝd/100)²` rather than resting on the floor of 400, and the censoring fraction must be reported per arm.
- **Threshold.** `h` arrives via `set_threshold(h)` from the harness's ARL_0 calibration under D-2a, per (environment, regime, confounder, distractor_level, delay, estimator) **and now per instance** (D-11.6). `h` is not transferable across cells, arms or instances.
- **Scope.** ARL-band attainment governs the **descriptive alarm outcomes only** (contract §G, R3-8). The primary and P2 are computed on all pairs regardless of band.

## 7. Probed variant, D-7a [N]

- **Stream.** Arm 3 runs on episodes carrying the **exact probe injections used by `seq_ibd`** — same balanced pre-randomised (actuator, sign) blocks of length `2K`, same probe RNG derivation, same eligibility mask, same replaced-action semantics. The two arms are matched transition-for-transition; only the estimator differs. The probe RNG derivation is owned by `sequential-ibd-spec.md` (round 5 found no seed specified anywhere and no channel to deliver one, R5-17); **this arm requires only that the derivation be declared there, that the injected stream be bitwise identical to arm 1's, and that its hash appear in both ledger rows.** Arm 3 never generates probes: `request_probe(steps) → None`.
- **Predictor.** **Bitwise identical `β, b_raw, μ, σ, l, degenerate, m_a, s_a, W_a, q`** to arm 2, from the same non-probed fault-free population (episodes 900–919). The score function is literally identical across arms 2 and 3, so the arm-3 − arm-2 difference isolates the effect of probes on the *stream*, not on the *fit*. **T-CMP-identity.**
- **Re-derived per arm:** `(ā, v, k)` on the probed stream, `h`, the isotonic map `g`, the operating point `θ*`. Probe injections replace the applied action with a ±unit vector, which changes the innovation distribution through clipping, delay and (family N) saturation; sharing `h` would confound "probes help" with "probes are out of the null".
- **Probe transitions and the Δ window [N] — the choice round 5 had to guess.** In the **fit** population, a probe transition and its `τ_max = 2` successors are dropped (§1.2). In **scoring**, a probe step is an **ordinary environment step**: it enters `r̃`, it enters `ã` through its **applied** action (which is the probe), it enters the trailing window, and it enters `𝒞*`'s maximum. No marking, no gap, no down-weighting, no window reset, no exclusion from `n`.
- **Declared consequence.** `Σ̂_a` is fitted on the non-probed split, so on a probed stream the realised action covariance is larger than the whitener assumes and Δ_c is inflated under the null as well as under the alternative. The inflation enters through the shared `ǎ_i`, so it is common to all channels and largely a level shift rather than a re-ordering, but **no claim is made that the ranking is unaffected**; the ledger's `probe_mag_over_action_rms` and `policy_action_rms` are the fields against which the size of the effect is read, and arm 3's own ARL_0 calibration absorbs the level shift in the alarm channel. Keeping `Σ̂_a` in `fit_predictor` is what preserves the bitwise-identity property above, which is the arm's reason to exist.
- **Budget.** Arm 3 is charged the full `probe_budget = 0.05` and task regret where a reward exists, so the cost comparison against `seq_ibd` is honest.
- **Sign blindness is a formula-level constraint.** Declared `sign_blindness = formula`. Arm 3 *does* see the probe sign — the sign **is** the applied action, a legitimate feature — and withholding it is impossible. The constraint is on the frozen statistic: **Δ, S and both returned statistics are functions of `(r̃, ǎ, l)` only; `l`, `m_a`, `s_a` and `W_a` are fitted offline on a non-probed split, and nothing conditions on `probe_flag` or on any randomisation-stratified grouping of the action.** Enforced by (i) the spec digest and source hash in the ledger; (ii) **T-CMP-marker** — permuting `probe_flag` leaves `(raw_support, secondary_support, p, stat, raise)` bitwise identical, which proves *marker* blindness only; (iii) **T-CMP-sign-metamorphic** — insert unit actions `±e_k` at scheduled steps of a fault-free stream with `probe_flag = False` and assert (a) outputs bitwise identical to the same stream with those steps flagged `True`, and (b) negating every inserted sign leaves `l`, `m_a`, `s_a`, `W_a` bitwise unchanged and Δ unchanged in distribution (paired two-sided test, declared tolerance, `n ≥ 400` windows). Note that (b) is stronger for Δ than it was for v2's mean shift: **Δ is invariant to the sign of the action only in distribution, not pathwise**, because ĉ_c is linear in ã — the correct statement, and the one T-CMP-sign-metamorphic now asserts.
- **What this arm is for.** It separates *having* interventions from *using them causally*. Round-4 reproductions put arm 3 within 0.01 AUC of arm 2 on the old statistic; **v3 makes no such prediction**, because Δ is a covariance statistic and probes raise the action variance that Δ divides by and multiplies through. The arm-3 − arm-2 difference is a round-6 measurement, stated in advance to be unpredicted.

## 8. Calibration tier [N] (secondary, contract §G tier 2)

- **Splits.** Per (instance, cell, arm): 24 episodes (ids 2000–2023, 12 event-carrying) to fit the calibrator, 16 disjoint episodes (ids 2100–2115, 8 event-carrying) to choose the operating point, all disjoint from the predictor-fit, alarm-reference, ARL and scored episodes. Both artefacts frozen before scoring.
- **Who fits it.** `g` needs oracle labels `1[c ∈ S^obs,ε]` and contract E5 puts the oracle in a process the estimator may never import, so **the harness fits `g` and `θ*` and injects them through `configure(bundle.calibrator_params)`**, hashed and logged. The estimator fits no calibrator.
- **Which statistic `g` maps [N] — an open choice v2 did not have to make.** `p_c` is the probability that `c ∈ S^obs,ε`, a **full-channel membership** quantity, and the primary never uses `p`. Therefore **`g` is fitted on the secondary statistic — that is, on `l`** — `p = g(secondary_support) = g(l)`, pooled over eligible steps and channels, and contract §G's P2 co-primary (mean `p_c` over oracle-labelled confounded distractors at `offsets_P2`) is computed from that `p`. **`raw_support` is never passed through `g`; the primary never passes through it at all.**
- **Consequence of a static secondary [N].** Because `l` is constant in `t`, `g` is effectively a **per-channel-value map**: within one (instance, cell, arm, confounder) the fit sees at most `C` distinct x-values, each repeated once per eligible step, so every channel's steps form one exact-tie block and `p_c` is the same number at every step of the scored episode — hence the same at `offsets_P2` = {200, 500, 1000}. The P2 co-primary for this arm is therefore a **static false-support reading**: it measures how much probability the frozen loading puts on confounded distractors, which is exactly the shared-cause failure §2 declares in advance and does not repair. It is reported that way, with the three offsets stated to be identical by construction rather than three independent readings.
- **Fit.** Non-decreasing PAVA on `(secondary_support_c = l_c, label_c(t))` pooled over **eligible** steps and channels of the 24 fit episodes, with exact-tie aggregation (identical values form one weighted block; with a static secondary a channel's eligible steps are all one such block, and channels whose rounded `l` coincides join it). Fitted values clamped into [0.001, 0.999] so log loss is finite.
- **Eligible step — window purity [N] (closes R5-16).** A step is eligible iff `t_ep ≥ n_warm` **and** its trailing window does not straddle the event: in an event-carrying episode, `t ≤ event_t − 1` (window entirely pre-event, labels `S^obs,ε(pre)`) or `t ≥ event_t + W − 1` (window entirely post-event, labels `S^obs,ε(post)`). At `W = 500`, `event_t = 1000`, `n_warm = 30`: **971 pure-pre steps (t = 29..999) + 501 pure-post steps (t = 1499..1999) = 1,472 eligible steps per event-carrying episode**, and **1,971 per event-free episode (t = 29..1999)**; total **12·1472 + 12·1971 = 41,316 eligible steps per channel per (instance, arm)**. Round 5 found this rule applied to the IBD arm only, so the two arms' calibrators were not like-for-like; the counts are stated here as that spec states its own. With a static secondary the rule no longer purifies the **statistic** — `l` has no window to straddle — but it still fixes **which label set** each channel-step carries (`S^obs,ε(pre)` or `(post)`) and keeps the label weights like-for-like with the IBD arm's calibrator; it is retained unchanged for those two reasons.
- **Apply.** Right-continuous step `g(x) = v[searchsorted(knots, x, "right") − 1]`, index clamped, output clipped to [0,1]. No interpolation, no smoothing.
- **Operating point.** `θ*` = the **largest** tied maximiser of micro-F1 on the 16-episode validation split only, never on the fit split. Frozen; reported F1 is at `θ*`.
- **Scoring.** Leave-one-instance-out with held-out event times; cross-fitted Brier and log loss of calibrated `p_c`; no "uncalibrated proper score".
- **The primary is untouched.** The primary AUC is on `raw_support` and is invariant to any monotone map, so it uses no supervised data at all.

## 10. Costs [N]

Probe steps 0 for arm 2; 0 requested and `probe_budget = 0.05` charged for arm 3. Per step: one dense mat-vec `φ_t β_raw` at `C·(C+3K+1)` multiply-adds; one `q × 3K` whitening mat-vec (36 at K = 2); one rank-1 add and one rank-1 subtract on the `C × q` accumulator (`2·C·q`, i.e. 288 at C = 24 and 1,368 at C = 114); one `C × q` norm; plus O(C) for standardisation, the rings, clipping, the sentinel write and the alarm maximum. ≈ 1.1·10³ at C = 24 and ≈ 1.5·10⁴ at C = 114 — the same order as the IBD arm's amortised ≈ 1.3·10⁴, so the cost comparison stays fair, and the Δ machinery adds ≈ 60 percent to v2's per-step cost at C = 24 and ≈ 15 percent at C = 114, not an order of magnitude. The `J^{(h)}` recursion and the eigendecomposition of `Σ̂_a` are `|ℋ|` `C×C` mat-mats and one `3K × 3K` symmetric eigensolve **once, at fit time**, never online. Memory: `β` ≈ 108 kB at C = 114, the `W = 500` rings ≈ 456 kB (r̃) + 24 kB (ǎ), the accumulator ≈ 5 kB, plus O(C) frozen vectors and the isotonic knots — ≈ 0.13 MB at C = 24 and ≈ 0.6 MB at C = 114. No growth in `t`. **Fitting cost under D-11.6** is now per instance and per confounder condition: 20 fit + 20 alarm-reference + 40 calibration episodes × 2 conditions × 10 instances × cells, at ≈ 0.25 s per 2,000-step episode on the reference interpreter (numpy 2.4.4, single core). The runtime pilot (contract §I, T-I) must price this before any calendar commitment; v2's per-cell fitting was ten times cheaper and D-11.6 is the reason it is not.

## 13. Pseudo-code [N]

```python
# frozen by fit_predictor: beta_raw, b_raw, mu[C], sd[C], l[C], degen[C], m_a[3K], s_a[3K], W_a[q,3K], q,
#                          sec_static[C]  (the SECONDARY statistic itself; static in t)
# by fit_alarm_reference: (abar, v, k);  by configure(): g, theta*;  by set_threshold(): h
# consts: LAM_REL=1e-4, LAM_A=1e-6, KAPPA_MIN=1e-4, W=500, Z_CAP=8, D_CAP=1e4, ABSENT=-(D_CAP+1), L_ABSENT=-1,
#         N_WARM=max(30, 5*3*K), SIG_FLOOR=1e-6, S_FLOOR=1e-8, SIG_DEG_REL=1e-2, L_DEG=1e-3,
#         KMULT=0.5, TAU_MAX=2, HORIZONS=(1,2,3), TIE_SIG=12

def fit_predictor(transitions):                 # arms 2 and 3 receive the SAME non-probed fault-free split
    tr   = drop_probe_and_successors(transitions, TAU_MAX)          # guard; no-op on the confirmatory split
    phi  = rows([o_t, a_t, a_{t-1}, a_{t-2}, 1]) within episodes, lags zero-filled at episode start
    Y    = rows(o_{t+1});  n = len(Y)
    m, s = phi[:, :-1].mean(0), maximum(phi[:, :-1].std(0, ddof=0), S_FLOOR)
    Xt   = hstack([(phi[:, :-1] - m) / s, ones(n, 1)])               # penalised cols have ||x||^2 == n
    D    = diag([1]*(C+3*K) + [0]);  lam = LAM_REL * n
    Bt   = cho_solve(Xt.T @ Xt + lam * D, Xt.T @ Y)                  # intercept unpenalised
    beta_raw, b_raw = Bt[:-1] / s[:, None], Bt[-1] - (m / s) @ Bt[:-1]
    R    = Y - Xt @ Bt
    mu, sd = R.mean(0), maximum(R.std(0, ddof=1), SIG_FLOOR)
    bo, ba = beta_raw[:C], [beta_raw[C+j*K : C+(j+1)*K] for j in (0, 1, 2)]
    J, l = zeros(K, C), zeros(C)
    for h in HORIZONS:                                               # iterated prediction, later actions == 0
        J = J @ bo + ba[h-1];  l = maximum(l, norm(J, axis=0) / sd)
    degen = (sd < SIG_DEG_REL * median(sd)) & (l < L_DEG)            # joint, relative, fit-time only
    m_a, s_a = m[C:C+3*K], s[C:C+3*K]                                # the SAME standardisation, action block
    At   = (phi[:, C:C+3*K] - m_a) / s_a                             # unit-variance columns by construction
    Sig  = (At.T @ At) / n + LAM_A * eye(3*K)                        # exact unit diagonal, then the ridge
    nu, V = eigh(Sig);  keep = nu >= KAPPA_MIN * nu.max();  q = keep.sum()
    W_a  = (V[:, keep] / sqrt(nu[keep])).T                           # q x 3K whitener (D-11.2a rank rule)
    sec_static = round_sig(l, TIE_SIG);  sec_static[degen] = L_ABSENT # SECONDARY = the frozen loading, l
                                                                      # unclipped, uncapped, never updated

def fit_alarm_reference(transitions):           # arm 2: ep 920-939 non-probed; arm 3: ep 940-959 PROBED
    m_stream = [max(abs(rtilde(tr))[~degen]) for tr in transitions]  # may change (abar, v, k) ONLY
    abar, v = mean(m_stream), std(m_stream, ddof=1);  k = abar + KMULT * v

def new_episode():
    S, t_ep, lag = 0.0, 0, [zeros(K), zeros(K)]
    ring_r, ring_a = empty_ring(W, C), empty_ring(W, q);  acc = zeros((C, q))

def update(tr):                                 # every environment step; probe steps included, unmarked
    if tr.t == 0 or tr.t != t_prev + 1: new_episode()
    t_ep += 1
    phi_t = concat([tr.prev_obs, tr.applied_action, lag[0], lag[1]])
    r     = tr.obs - (phi_t @ beta_raw + b_raw)
    a_std = (phi_t[C:C+3*K] - m_a) / s_a;  a_w = W_a @ a_std          # standardise, then whiten
    lag   = [tr.applied_action, lag[0]]
    rt    = clip((r - mu) / sd, -Z_CAP, Z_CAP)
    if ring_r.full():                                                 # drop the leaving transition
        r_old, a_old = ring_r.pop(), ring_a.pop();  acc -= outer(r_old, a_old)
    ring_r.push(rt);  ring_a.push(a_w);  acc += outer(rt, a_w)        # acc = sum over the window
    n     = min(W, t_ep)
    Delta = zeros(C) if t_ep < N_WARM else sqrt(n) * norm(acc / n, axis=1)     # D-11.2a, vector over C
    Dhat  = minimum(Delta, D_CAP)
    raw   = round_sig(-Dhat, TIE_SIG)                                 # PRIMARY: higher = still controlled
    raw[degen] = ABSENT                                               # primary sentinel only; sec has its own
    S     = max(0.0, S + max(abs(rt)[~degen]) - k)                    # CUSUM on max_c |r~| over C*
    return (raw, g(sec_static), S, 1 if S > h else 0)                 # harness owns persistence and refractory

def secondary_support():  return sec_static                           # static in t; never sees the event
```

## 14. Claims we do not make [N]

An **engineering baseline**: the strongest passive reading we could find at the capacity the contract permits, frozen on principle so the confirmatory margin is decided by the science and not by the baseline's specification. We do **not** claim it is the strongest possible passive comparator, the best linear residual monitor, or optimal in any sense.

**We delete v2's claim that its change statistic was competent.** v2 §3 argued that the innovation-mean shift was required as a change term and never checked that subtracting it helped. It did not: round 5 measured the specified score as **strictly dominated by its own loading term on every cell** — comparator AUC with the confounder absent 0.64–0.83 against the D-10.3 floor of 0.85, while the loading alone reached 0.99 pre-event and the shift term cost 0.12–0.27 AUC. The cause is structural, not a tuning matter: under a centred policy an actuator loss changes the residual's covariance with the action, not its mean. Those numbers stand as the record of why v3 exists (§15) and are **not** carried forward as v3 performance claims. Nothing in v2 §3 survives except the window and the warm-up.

No identifiability, consistency, FDR-control, size or ARL-optimality claim. Δ_c is a score-*type* statistic, not a level-α test: the innovations are neither independent nor Gaussian under the closed-loop policy, the χ²_{3K} reference is asymptotic and is used only to state the null scale, and Δ is consumed as a ranking statistic and an alarm input. The ARL_0 is *calibrated*, not derived. `J^{(h)}` is an **open-loop sensitivity of the fitted predictor**, not a closed-loop sensitivity and not an estimate of the causal Jacobian `M` (C4); that it recovers reachable structure on family L is an empirical convenience, not an identification result. Neither statistic estimates `e_j`, `R`, `M` or body membership (C6) — only a ranking. We do **not** claim above-chance ranking under confounding: §2 says in advance that confounded distractors keep a large loading, which is reported as a result, not repaired. We do **not** claim Δ sees every removal: §3.4 declares in advance that Δ fires on retained channels that drew on the lost actuator, and that a purely downstream loss is seen an order of magnitude more weakly. We do **not** predict the arm-3 − arm-2 difference: probes change the action variance Δ is built on, and §7 states that the comparison is unpredicted rather than borrowing round 4's ≈ 0.01 AUC. We do **not** claim v3 clears the D-10.3 competence floor: every number in this document is a single-instance diagnostic, and the floor is a per-cell interval-bounded quantity that **round 6 must execute**. We make **no** performance claim for the secondary full-channel AUC. It is the frozen loading `l`, it never sees the event, and the contract attaches no floor to it; §4.2 states in advance what it will and will not report — a large loading on confounded distractors (false support, the shared-cause failure the benchmark measures), low loadings on unconfounded distractors and pads, and a stale loading on the lost channel that keeps it ranked as supported and costs the secondary a little after the event. We do **not** carry v1's or v2's measured numbers forward as performance claims. Finally, we disclose that this specification and the primary metric were frozen **after** pilot simulations had shown the interventional arm clears them, and that `λ_rel`'s value was measured as the best of a small grid in round 5 on the superseded statistic (contract §G Disclosure); confirmation uses fresh, sealed seeds and fresh instance draws (D-11.3), and the paper states this as a limitation.

## 16. Observations on contract v3.9, reported and not fixed [I]

This document implements contract v3.9 as written. Three statements are recorded here for the author: two that do not survive contact with the frozen generator and are reported rather than repaired, and one (C-2) that the contract has already resolved.

- **C-1. "A fit-time constant … scores exactly 0.5" (§G, primary outcome) is true only of a statistic constant across channels.** A fit-time-constant *vector* — such as this arm's `l`, or the static-structure control the same paragraph mandates — has a fixed but non-neutral ranking within the pre-event support, and CL-4-by-construction biases it: the lost channel is the dominant body component of the event actuator, which carries near-maximal loading, so the static control ranks it *high* and scores *below* chance. *Single-instance diagnostic*: 0.200 at τ = 0 and 0.000 at τ = 2 on seed 0, present and absent. The harness assertion "the static-structure control must score 0.5 ± Monte Carlo in every cell" will therefore fail as written. Either the control must be a channel-constant statistic, or the assertion must become a *reported* quantity with the CL-4 bias stated.
- **C-2. Resolved by contract change before the freeze.** v3 as first drafted implemented the contract's then-secondary `l_c − Δ_c` and reported that it could not carry what §G asks of the secondary: Δ after the event is one to two orders of magnitude larger than `l` on every action-coupled channel, so the difference ranks the true support below the null distractors — *single-instance diagnostic* 0.063 present and 0.189 absent at offset 500, against `l` alone at 0.800 and 0.958. Contract v3.9 §H(2) now sets `secondary_support_c = l_c`, the frozen H-horizon loading alone, and records `l_c − Δ_c` as considered and rejected **before the freeze**; §4.2 and §4.4 of this document are aligned with that. The rejected variant, its numbers, and the diagnostic that produced them (`superseded-specs/comparator-v3-diagnostic.py` and its output) are **retained as the record of why**. The change rests on the structural argument stated in advance — a difference of two quantities on incommensurable scales is dominated by the larger — and not on an outcome: no clipping, rescaling or re-weighting was fitted to rescue the difference, and the secondary that replaces it has no free constant at all.
- **C-3. The D-11.5 clock is symmetric in rule and asymmetric in effect, and the D-2a cap is where that surfaces.** Charging the prefix to the arm that needs it is the honest rule, but it gives the two confirmatory arms different dispersion tolerance under one cap (round 5: sd ≤ 1861 against sd ≤ 2282), and D-2a's floor of 400 run lengths is sufficient only if the run-length sd ≤ 1020. §0's D-2a row should say what happens when the cap binds for one arm and not the other; contract §G's partial-order endpoint covers the reporting but not the calibration.
