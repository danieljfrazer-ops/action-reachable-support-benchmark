## A0. Reference generator [N] (D-10.1, D-11.3, D-11.4)

All instances used in simulations, pilots and confirmation are drawn by `reference_generator.py` from the frozen configuration family, **family L or family N selected by `cfg["family"]`** with one shared configuration stream (so an L and an N instance at the same seed share A_b, B, C_d, G, W_o, W_u): A_b spectral radius 0.85 with declared sparsity; B dense with |entries| ≥ c_min except the CL-4 row; C_d; A_d, A_w, A_x diagonal AR(1) at 0.9; policy W_o supported on body channels only with declared gain; W_u scaled to meet ρ_min; G on the confounded half of x; four padding channels; **no copies** in confirmatory instances (D-10.6); zero initial state with burn-in; noise keyed by (seed, variable, t). Each instance is certified (family L: closed-loop radius on the delay-augmented state ≤ ρ_cl; family N: **T-L9b** empirical boundedness and four-group stationary-mean agreement, §B; both: saturation, ρ_min witness, ρ severed under randomised do(a), operational-effect margin > 0 on every reachable latent, faithfulness bound |entry| ≥ c_min on nonzero entries of A_b and B, label consistency E1b, and **CL-4 by construction and by certification: the dominant body component of actuator `event_actuator` is driven by no other actuator and fed by no other body component, and the confirmatory event is certified to remove ≥ 1 observed channel from S^obs,ε**) or resampled at the next sub-seed. `draw_certified` stores S^obs,ε(pre) at draw time (`S_obs_pre_event`); every `apply_event` invalidates the family-N stationary mean so post-event labels are re-certified (CL-8). Noise streams are keyed by a fixed integer namespace, never by a Python string hash. Burn-in of 2,000 steps precedes every episode from a separately keyed prefix. Configuration seeds 0 to 9 are development instances and are exhausted for confirmation; the confirmation instance set is derived from a sealed secret (registry row *confirmation seeds*, `confirmation_seeds.py`, T-seeds) and is neither derived nor inspected until specs and constants are frozen (D-11.3). **Family N is implemented and certified but stays out of the confirmatory conjunction until a cross-model round reproduces it (D-11.4a)**; family-N cells are run and reported in round 6 as a reproduction target, not as a gate. Three independent implementations of either arm on the same configuration seed must agree to Monte Carlo error; this is the exit condition for design review, checked on the configuration set and the perturbation set.

## B. Structural equations [N]

Family L:
- u_t = ρ_u u_{t−1} + ε^u_t
- a_t = clip(W_o o_t + W_u u_t + ε^a_t) under the default policy; a_t := a* under do(a_t = a*)
- b_{t+1} = A_b b_t + B_t a_{t−τ} + ε^b_t
- d_{t+1} = A_d d_t + C_d b_t + ε^d_t
- w_{t+1} = A_w w_t + ε^w_t
- x_{t+1} = A_x x_t + G u_t + ε^x_t (no action parent; structural fact, tested by graph inspection T-E1a)
- o_t = avail_t ⊙ gain_t ⊙ (Assign_t z_t) + ε^o_t, Assign_t ∈ {0,1}^{C×N_z} with at most one 1 per row

Family N (**implemented, D-11.4a**): b_{t+1} = A_b b_t + tanh(B_t a_{t−τ} / s) · s + κ · clip(b_t ⊙ b_t, −m, m) + ε^b_t, elementwise, with s = 1.0, κ = 0.1, m = 4.0 from the registry; d, w, x, the policy and the observation map are exactly family L's (`Instance._body_drive`). Family N is required to be **empirically bounded and stationary**, not contractive (CX-09). **T-L9b** (`boundedness_certificate`, test_gen_family_N_*): 32 chains from initial states uniform in [−4, 4]^{N_z} under the default closed-loop policy for 20,000 steps must satisfy |z| ≤ 100 at every step, and the stationary means of the b and d blocks (the nonlinear loop; w and x are linear AR(1) with exact zero mean) estimated on four groups of eight chains after discarding the first 2,000 steps must agree within δ_inv = 0.05 (max over latents of the group range); instances failing either are resampled. Disclosed consequence of the quadratic term: it is non-negative, so certified family-N instances have **nonzero stationary offsets** in b and, amplified through C_d/(1 − ρ_ar), in d (order 1 to 40 on development seeds); sub-seeds whose offsets exceed the bound are rejected (test_gen_family_N_divergent_*). No Lipschitz claim is made. With κ = 0 and s → ∞ the dynamics reduce to family L to 1e-6 (test_gen_family_N_reduces_*).

Stability [N] (L9, CL-9, FB-17, CX-03, GM2-3): spectral radius of A_b, A_d, A_w, A_x ≤ ρ_max; **closed-loop radius on the delay-augmented state** [b_t, …, b_{t−τ}; d_t; a_{t−1}, …, a_{t−τ}] with the policy's actual observed channels ≤ ρ_cl, certified at sampling and after every event (family N: T-L9b empirical boundedness and stationarity on the same closed loop replaces the radius, which is reported for the linearised loop only); |ρ_u| < 1; actions clipped; saturation fraction logged and **instances with saturation fraction > 0.05 are rejected** (OP-16); ρ_min certified after W_o, W_u are sampled; sampling rejects violations.
Faithfulness [N] (G5): nonzero entries of A_b, B_t have |entry| ≥ c_min; after sampling and after **every change event**, T-E1b checks that structurally reachable components have zero-noise open-loop responses above ε_faith; otherwise resample.
Common random numbers [N]: noise indexed by (seed, variable, t).

## C. Ground-truth objects [N]

C1 **Structural latent support** S^latent_{t,H}: components of z = [b; d; w; x] with a directed path from a_t within H steps, **counting delay τ so the first hit is at h = τ + 1** (empty for H ≤ τ), by graph reachability on the sign pattern of the full block adjacency (A_b, B_t, C_d, A_d, A_w, A_x). Exact. Reference `structural_reach_full`; tests T-C1-delay, T-C1-chain, T-K10.

C2 **Operational latent effect** e_{j,t} = max_{h≤H, a∈𝒜} ‖E[z_{j,t+h} | do(a_t = a, later actions 0)] − E[z_{j,t+h} | do(0)]‖ from the stationary state z̄ (family L: z̄ = 0 and the zero-noise response is exact; family N: z̄ is the empirical stationary mean `Instance.zbar`, estimated on a 4,000-step chain after the burn-in prefix and **re-estimated after every event**), estimated by n_oracle paired samples with common random numbers where noise enters. The oracle reports e_j with a simultaneous 99 percent interval over all (component, horizon, probe) triples by Bonferroni on paired-difference t-intervals (CX-15); an instance whose interval for any channel contains ε is **rejected and resampled**; certification is **repeated after every change event** (CL-8); the resampling rate and the distribution of min_j |e_j − ε| are logged and reported in the paper as a limitation (OP-23); `n_resamples` per instance enters the config hash; an appendix cell keeps near-threshold instances and scores them separately; a generator configuration with rejection rate above 20 percent is itself rejected (L4). Labels are "structurally exact, numerically certified".

C3 **Operational observed support** (L5): S^obs,ε_{t,H} = { c : avail_t(c) = 1, Assign_t(c) = j ≠ ∅, |gain_t(c)| · e_{j,t} > ε }. Gain is part of the definition; a gain event may change S^obs,ε. Test T-C3, T-L5. **Pre- and post-event support (D-11.1):** S^obs,ε(pre) is the support certified at draw time before the confirmatory event (`S_obs_pre_event`); S^obs,ε(post) the support re-certified after it. The **primary support set** is S^obs,ε(pre); the **lost set** is S^obs,ε(pre) \ S^obs,ε(post), certified non-empty by CL-4. Under complete actuator loss the support cannot grow (test_gen_actuator_loss_*).

**Observation clock (R4-CX-08).** A transition at step t carries (prev_obs = o_t, applied_action = a_t, obs = o_{t+1}). An increment anchored at a probe applied at step t_p is o_{t_p + h} − o_{t_p}, with o_{t_p} the PRE-action observation, so the first hit is at h = τ + 1. Gate test T-IBD-horizon: a deterministic impulse must first move the increment at h = τ + 1.

C4 **Open-loop response** R_{t,h}(a) = E[b_{t+h} | do(a_t = a, a_{t+1..t+h−1} = 0), z_t = z̄] − E[b_{t+h} | do(0, ..., 0), z_t = z̄], a ∈ 𝒜, h ∈ ℋ, z̄ the stationary mean estimated after burn_in (family L: z̄ = 0 is exact; family N: the empirical z̄ is used and the response is not odd in a, T-C4-N). **R is indexed by all |𝒜| = 2K probes in the frozen order (+e_1, −e_1, …, +e_K, −e_K)**; the Jacobian M is a separate object exposed for family L only (CL-7, CX-04). Family L closed form: M_{t,h} = 0 for h ≤ τ, A_b^{h−1−τ} B_t for h > τ. Tests T-G1, T-G2.

C5 **Observation map** P_t = (Assign_t, gain_t, avail_t). Copies are scored as equivalence classes wherever they occur; confirmatory instances contain no copies (D-10.6 pending confirmation), so the primary AUC over observation channels and the equivalence-class rule coincide there; copies are exercised in acceptance fixtures.

C6 **Estimand naming** (L13): the primary quantity is **action-reachable observation support**, not body membership. Body membership B^obs (channels assigned to b) is an oracle-only construct reported in an appendix and never inferred from reachability. Downstream channels d are inside S and outside B by design; examples must include one.

## D. Events and schedules [N]

Events with their **computed** effect on the four objects (the oracle decides; the table is a prediction checked by T-D):

| Event | Mechanism | Predicted effect |
|---|---|---|
| actuator remap / gain | B_t ← Π B_t or D B_t | R changes; S^latent generically unchanged |
| actuator loss, complete | column k of B_t ← 0 | S changes iff reachability changes (coupling, horizon); R changes |
| actuator loss, partial | column k of B_t ← γ · column, γ_min ≤ γ < 1 | R changes; S unchanged (structural); post-event re-certification guarantees S^obs,ε unchanged (CL-8) |
| sensor dropout | avail_t(c) ← 0 | S^obs changes iff c ∈ S^obs; P changes |
| sensor swap | rows c1, c2 of Assign_t exchanged | S^obs changes iff exactly one of c1, c2 ∈ S^obs; P changes |
| sensor gain | gain_t(c) ← g′ | P changes; S^obs,ε changes iff |g′| e_j crosses ε (T-L5 covers 0, near-0, sign flip) |
| morphology | N_b changes with padding | all may change |

Primary-cell instance constraint (CL-4, **certified by construction since round 5**): for confirmatory cells the lost actuator's column must reach ≥ 1 component with no alternative path within H, so that S^obs,ε changes; the generator enforces it structurally (A0), the oracle certifies it (`certification["s_change"]`, `n_lost`), and `confirmation-design.csv` carries `s_change_required`. Disclosed: on development seeds the event typically removes **exactly one** observed channel (the dominant body component of actuator 0), so the per-episode primary is an AUC with one negative against the remaining pre-event-support channels and takes coarse values; the confirmatory margin is assessed on intervals clustered by instance, never on a single episode. Whether CL-4 should require n_lost ≥ 2 is an open round-6 question, not a decision taken here.
Schedules (spacing ≥ event_spacing, T-G-spacing; administrative censoring is thereby excluded): single unannounced change; ABA, ABC with equal dwell and transition counts; A′ (unseen, structurally matched); Poisson and bursty held-out families, generated and hashed before development ends.

## E. Correctness gate [N]

E1 **Structural tests** (exact): T-E1a graph has no action ancestor for x or w and has one for d; T-E1b two independent label derivations (graph reachability; zero-noise finite-difference) agree on every sampled instance and after every event.
E2 **Statistical tests** (tolerance, power, n_oracle declared): T-E2a invariance for non-reachable components (mean-difference, valid for constant probes); T-E2b sensitivity for reachable components; T-E2c confounding present observationally (T = T_E2): a generated witness pair (k, j) with |corr(a_k, x_j)| ≥ ρ_min, both columns non-degenerate; zero-variance non-witness pairs are skipped, not failed (CX-14); a fraction f_conf of distractor channels are children of u and the median |corr| over confounded channels is reported (GM-7); T-E2d confounding severed under randomised do(a ~ Uniform(𝒜)), T = T_E2: max pairwise |corr| ≤ max(δ_inv, 3·sqrt(2 ln(K·N_x) / T)) (multiplicity-aware, OP-24); constant probes return NaN and are rejected by the test, never silently passed.
E3 **Mutants** (each must make ≥ 1 test fail; `mutants.py` runs them and exits non-zero on any survivor): executable today: M1 leak a into x; M7 negative-power Jacobian; M8 gain ignored; delay cap (Codex); 999·B delayed Jacobian (Gemini); skip-H2 reachability (Gemini); no-delay and no-downstream reachability (Claude); censoring excluded from HPDT. **Uncovered until the generator and oracle exist:** M2 swap bookkeeping; M3 loss on observation; M4 unpaired noise; M5 oracle reads event type; M6 evaluator readable (CL-3).
E4 **Determinism**: same host bitwise; cross host within tolerance with identical config hash.
E5 **Isolation**: oracle in a separate process; candidate receives serialised observations only; canary field test.
E6 **Independent acceptance** (L16): a black-box acceptance suite written from `interface-spec-v5.md` (a one-page frozen interface), not from the implementation; plus one hand-computed rectangular, noisy case exercising every outcome class (detected, missed, terminated) that Daniel checks against both the pipeline and `contract_ref.py` (OP-31).

## F. Estimator interface [N]

Per step: p_c ∈ [0,1] per channel (probability c ∈ S^obs,ε; from a declared probabilistic model or a calibrator fitted on a separate split; logged); α^S_t ∈ {0,1}. Optional, scored only in later phases or appendices: α^R_t, R̂, assignment distributions. **Paper 1 scores p_c and α^S only.** Every baseline declares its **information set** (L10): which channels, whether oracle coordinates or P are visible, whether probe data may train the controller. The estimator receives full transitions (`interface-spec-v5.md`), including the applied action and probe marker (CX-03). **Fit hierarchy (D-11.6):** every learned artefact of every arm (predictor β, μ, σ, l; alarm reference ā, v, k; calibrator g; operating point θ*; threshold h) is fitted **per instance** on that instance's own fault-free fit split, drawn with episode seeds disjoint from every scored episode and declared in the arm's spec; nothing is pooled across instances or across confounder arms; the ledger records the key (cell, instance_seed, arm) on every artefact and the fit-split episode seeds. `update` returns the arm's **primary statistic** `raw_support[C]` (the change-tracking statistic the primary AUC is computed on) and the harness reads the arm's **secondary full-channel statistic** through `secondary_support() -> [C]` after each `update` (interface v5); an arm whose two statistics coincide returns the same vector. **Misspecification registry** (CX-18): R1 transforms are frozen and hashed: family L drift = parameters of the residual model fitted on pre-drift data then held while A_b, B_t are perturbed by +10 percent on the diagonal; family N = linear residual model fitted on family N data; T2 = linear autoregressive residual model. R0 on T2 (CL-5, GM-5) = an MLP residual model trained to convergence on 200,000 fault-free in-distribution steps with a Ljung-Box whiteness test on innovations at the pilot.

## G. Metrics [N]

- **Persistence ownership (R3-10, CX-05):** estimators emit the raw threshold exceedance each step; the harness owns persistence p and refractory r. For epoch-quantised estimators p is applied in epochs and the unit is recorded in the ledger. Alarm counted after p consecutive raises; refractory r. **Detection** = first counted alarm with event_t < alarm_t ≤ event_t + H_det. w_T is used only to match alarms to the nearest event when several exist (F5; the merge rule is deleted).
- **Confounder absent (F7, OP-21)** = the **same instance draw and the same noise streams** as the matching present cell, with G = 0 and W_u unchanged; present/absent differences are within-instance contrasts.
- **Detection outcome per event**: detected within H_det steps, or censored (missed, next event, episode end, early termination). Early termination is a competing outcome and is reported, never excluded (L11).
- **Primary outcome (D-11.1a; replaces D-9.2's full-channel ranking).** **Adaptation to change, scored over the pre-event support:** at each offset in offsets_rank (primary 500) after the confirmatory event, the threshold-free mid-rank AUC of the estimator's `raw_support` **restricted to S^obs,ε(pre)**, positives = channels still in S^obs,ε(post), negatives = the lost set. Reference `contract_ref.auc_pre_event_support` (T-G-pre-event; mutants D11-M1..M3). Properties: a fit-time constant, or any statistic that never sees the scored episode, scores exactly 0.5; channels the actions never controlled (distractors, world, padding) do not enter; invariant to any monotone calibrator, so it uses no supervised data. Scored per episode, paired by instance and cell; intervals clustered by instance; cells never pooled. **Secondary full-channel outcome (formerly the primary):** the same AUC of `secondary_support()` over all C channels against S^obs,ε(post), co-reported with AUPRC and prevalence and the grouped AUPRC (sensitivity, not a gate); it is where the loading term and the static-structure competence are reported (for the comparator, `secondary_support = l_c`; for the IBD arm, a_c). **Static-structure control (round 5, Opus), stated precisely:** a statistic that is constant **across the channels** of S^obs,ε(pre) scores exactly 0.5 (harness assertion, T-G-pre-event). A per-channel **vector** fixed before the scored episode (the static loading l_c, or any pre-event ranking) does not score 0.5; it scores whatever its own pre-event ordering gives, and on the frozen generator that is **below chance**, because CL-4 is enforced by making the lost channel the dominant body component of actuator 0, typically among the strongest pre-event channels (single-model diagnostic in `sequential-ibd-spec.md` draft 5: mean 0.21 over 20 draws). The static-vector control is therefore retained as a **descriptive arm whose AUC is reported**, predicted below 0.5 and disclosed as a property of the generator's CL-4 construction; it is not a harness assertion.
- **Offset read rule, one for every arm:** the statistic scored at offset Δ is the arm's output as of its **last emission whose transition index t ≤ event_t + Δ − 1** (the last `update` call that can precede step event_t + Δ; `Instance.run` iterates t = 0..T−1 and there is no transition at t = episode_len). For a step arm that is the update at t = event_t + Δ − 1 (1199, 1499, 1999); for the epoch arm on the grid t ≡ 2 (mod 20) it is 1182, 1482, 1982. The two arms are therefore read up to 17 steps apart at every offset; this is declared, and the paired difference Δ_AUC uses each arm's own read.
- **Co-primary (D-5, locked): confounded-channel false support** as before, tested hierarchically after the primary.
- **Secondary (D-9.5 tier 2): supervised calibration and operating-point transfer.** Isotonic calibrator fitted on the 24-episode fit split, operating point chosen on the separate 16-episode validation split, both frozen before scoring; F1 at that operating point; cross-fitted Brier and log loss of the calibrated probabilities; evaluated leave-one-instance-out with held-out event times. No "uncalibrated proper score" is reported.
- **Decision rules (D-9.3, D-10.3, on the D-11.1 primary).** Let Δ_AUC = AUC_pre(seq-IBD) − AUC_pre(passive comparator) per episode on the pre-event-support primary, paired within instance and cell, intervals clustered by instance. **Confounding benefit** = [Δ_AUC(present) − Δ_AUC(absent)] within regime: superiority if the lower 95 percent bound > δ_AUC on both SCM families and the sign holds on both control-tier environments; futility if the upper bound < δ_AUC on either SCM family; inconclusive otherwise. **R0-present** is a positive control: lower bound of Δ_AUC(present) > δ_AUC expected; failure is an anomaly that stops the phase. **R0-absent** is a comparator-competence validity check (D-10.3): in every required (environment, distractor_level, delay) cell the lower 95 percent bound of the comparator's **pre-event-support primary AUC** without confounding must be ≥ 0.85; Δ_AUC(absent) is reported. If the floor fails in any cell, the comparator specification, not the rule, is defective and the phase stops (round 5 stopped here on the old statistic; D-11.2 is the repair, to be executed in round 6). The secondary full-channel AUC carries no floor. The double-difference interaction is descriptive. Prevalence-adjusted AUPRC repeats each rule as a sensitivity, not a gate. The pilot's power arm sizes seeds for this conjunction (v4.4 H2 applies to these rules).
- **Disclosure.** δ_AUC, the comparator reading (H) and the primary metric were frozen after pilot simulations (rounds 3 and D-9) had shown the interventional arm clears them; confirmation uses fresh seeds and instance draws and the paper states this as a limitation.
- **Descriptive delay outcome** (formerly primary): **horizon-penalised detection time** HPDT = mean over events of min(delay, H_det) for detected events and H_det for missed or terminated events (a composite outcome by decision, not a restricted mean over right-censored times). Administrative censoring cannot occur because schedules satisfy event_spacing (T-G-spacing). Detection probability within H_det and the early-termination rate are reported alongside. (P2) Co-primary outcome (locked, D-5; CX-07, GM2-6): **confounded-channel false support** = mean p_c over oracle-labelled confounded distractor channels at offsets_P2 = {200, 500, 1000} after the event (D-10.5; the earlier {10, 50, 200} measured a pre-event quantity, R4-8); unconfounded distractors reported as a negative control; each offset reported with the fraction of runs for which it exists; the estimator-to-p_c mapping is a frozen isotonic calibrator fitted on the calibration split (FB-18). For the channel-agnostic CUSUM arm, p_c is derived from per-channel innovation statistics through the same calibrator (OP-13).
- **Alarm exposure (D-11.5).** Both arms count elapsed environment steps from reset; the fresh-start run length is `contract_ref.run_length_from_reset` (first counted alarm time, or the stream end with a censoring flag) and **includes** any warm-up prefix the arm needs. The harness's D-2a protocol therefore uses event-free calibration streams long enough for the prefix plus ≈ 3 × ARL_0, reports the censoring fraction, and never subtracts the prefix. Persistence is applied by the harness in the arm's declared unit (`persistence_unit`: `steps` or `epochs`, counted over the arm's emissions) and refractory in environment steps, through `contract_ref.count_alarms_timed(raises, times, p, r)` (T-G-timed; mutants D11-M4..M7); `count_alarms` is its step-cadence special case.
- **False-alarm control**: thresholds calibrated on a separate calibration split to a predeclared ARL_0; the **descriptive alarm comparison** is made at matched ARL_0, so operating-curve supports cannot be disjoint. (The primary is ARL-independent.) Full delay-versus-log-ARL curves reported descriptively; pAUC secondary only; disjoint curves reported as a partial order, never as a scalar.
- Support F1 at fixed offsets via out-of-band probes; Brier and log loss raw and calibrated; calibration split by whole configuration and schedule; ECE diagnostic with fixed bins.
- **Alarm matching (OP-12, R3-9):** two stages in `contract_ref.py`, both tested: `count_alarms_timed(raises, times, p, r) -> counted alarm times` (p consecutive emissions; refractory r steps; `count_alarms(raw, p, r)` for step arms) and `match_alarms(alarm_times, event_times, H_det, episode_end) -> (delays, outcomes, false_alarms)`; an alarm at delay d ∈ (0, H_det] after event e and before the next event is attributed to e.
- **Scope of ARL eligibility (R3-8, CX-08):** ARL-band attainment governs the descriptive alarm outcomes only; P1 and P2 are computed on all pairs regardless of band, with band attainment recorded as a covariate.
- **Pairwise handling of out-of-band methods (OP-11), descriptive outcomes only:** if either arm of a (seed, cell) pair is outside the ARL band, the pair is reported in the partial-order endpoint, the exclusion rate is reported by regime and arm, a maximum tolerable rate of 10 percent is predeclared (above it the **descriptive alarm comparison** is inconclusive; the primary is unaffected), and a sensitivity analysis imputes such pairs at H_det.
- **Per-environment outcome classes (OP-29/30):** Pendulum-v1 and PointMass2D never terminate, so `terminated` is structurally zero there and is reported as such; offsets that do not exist for a run are reported with their denominator.
- Costs: probes (in probe steps, see probe_budget), samples, operations; task regret only where a reward is declared.
- **Out-of-band scoring queries** (CX-17): the evaluator scores support via a snapshot-and-branch API with common random numbers; the live environment state, RNG and estimator bytes are asserted unchanged after scoring (T-OOB).

## H. Baselines for Stage 0A [N]

**Confirmatory arms (D-3a, D-7a, D-9.1).** (1) **Sequential IBD**, the **sign-randomised contrast**: per (channel c, horizon h, actuator k), a tie-corrected rank-sum z of the signed increments o_c(t+h) − o_c(t) after probes that used +e_k against those after probes that used −e_k, over a trailing window; a_c = max over (k, h) of |z|; no task-policy null; n_min_sign applies; `raw_support = secondary_support = a_c`; its alarm clock runs from reset and its warm-up prefix is charged to it (D-11.5); specified in `sequential-ibd-spec.md` draft 5. (2) **Passive comparator** (D-9.4, D-10.2): a channel-agnostic linear predictor with delay-aware features o_{t+1} = [o_t, a_t, a_{t−1}, a_{t−2}, 1]β (τ_max = 2), all penalised features standardised on the fit split before the ridge, fitted on fault-free in-distribution calibration episodes; the support baseline is the **H-horizon action loading** l_c = max over h ∈ ℋ of the standardised iterated-prediction sensitivity of channel c to the action (so downstream channels are not scored zero by construction); the round-4 one-lag comparator is retained as a labelled legacy arm in the appendix only; **change statistic (D-11.2a, replaces the innovation-mean shift, which round 5 showed cannot see an actuator loss under a centred policy):** with r̃_{i,c} the standardised innovations of the frozen predictor and ã_i ∈ R^{3K} the lagged-action features [a_i, a_{i−1}, a_{i−2}] standardised with the fit-split mean and scale, the **action-residual covariance score** over the trailing window of n = min(W, t_ep) steps is Δ_c(t) = sqrt( n · ĉ_cᵀ Σ̂_a^{−1} ĉ_c ), ĉ_c = (1/n) Σ_i r̃_{i,c} ã_i, Σ̂_a the fit-split covariance of ã (regularised as the spec states); under the null the innovations are uncorrelated with the actions and Δ_c is O(√(3K)); after a loss of actuator k the frozen model keeps predicting the k-column's effect, so the residual acquires covariance with a_{i−τ,k} and Δ_c grows as √n. **Primary statistic** `raw_support_c = −Δ_c` (higher = still controlled); **secondary full-channel statistic** `secondary_support_c = l_c`, the frozen H-horizon loading alone (the loading term retained for the secondary, D-11.2; it is the static-structure reading and is expected to keep its confounded false support and to lose a little on the lost channel after the event). The difference l_c − Δ_c was considered for the secondary and rejected before the freeze: after the event Δ_c exceeds l_c by one to two orders of magnitude on every action-coupled channel, so the difference ranks the true support below the distractors (comparator spec v3 diagnostic, secondary AUC 0.06 to 0.19 against 0.80 to 0.96 for l alone); any rescaling that rescued it would be chosen against the outcome. Both statistics clipped as the spec states; arm-specific isotonic calibrator; CUSUM on max_c |r̃| for the descriptive alarm channel. The spec fixes every remaining choice (window, regularisation, warm-up, clipping, degenerate channels) so that three implementations agree to Monte Carlo error. Full normative fields in `comparator-spec.md` v3. (3) **Probed comparator** (D-7a): the same predictor, score and CUSUM on streams carrying the identical randomised probe injections (same schedule and seed) as arm 1, with its own calibration because probe shocks change the null; separates "having interventions" from "using them causally". Arm 2 vs arm 1 is the headline; arm 3 vs arm 1 is the mechanism control. R0 = the linear predictor fitted on in-distribution fault-free data (correct only on family L; on family N and T2 it is the best linear model). R1 = the same predictor class under the frozen misspecification transform. The words "correct model" are dropped everywhere. Sequential IBD is specified in `sequential-ibd-spec.md` (D-4a), normative once signed by Daniel. Every estimator's `update` returns a **continuous statistic** alongside the binary alarm so the harness can calibrate a threshold (OP-4; interface v5). **Every arm fits per instance (D-11.6).**

## H2. Control-tier environments for Paper 1 [N] (D-1a)

- **PointMass2D** (new, in-repo, numpy): a 2-D point mass with two independent thrusters (K = 2), mass 1, linear drag 0.1, dt 0.05, continuous actions clipped to [−a_max, a_max]; body channels = (x, y, vx, vy); no termination; episode per registry. The axes are uncoupled, so losing thruster k removes (x_k, v_k) from the reachable support without ending the episode. Confounded distractors and exogenous channels are appended exactly as in the SCM. Ground truth by construction and certified by the oracle.
- **Pendulum-v1** (Gymnasium): one continuous torque in [−2, 2]; `TimeLimit` replaced by the registry episode length (disclosed); no natural termination; complete loss empties the support without termination. Distractors appended as above.
- CartPole is **not** used (FB-7, OP-6/7): a single-actuator balancing task terminates under complete loss and has a discrete action space.
