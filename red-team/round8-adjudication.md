# Round 8 adjudication (prose identifiability derivation, 9 September 2026)

Inputs: `review8-codex/derivation.md`, `review8-gemini/derivation.md`, `review8-claude-opus/derivation.md` (all three derived the closed-loop second-order statistics of the family L-C plant and the population limits of both arms; Claude and Gemini checked their algebra on toy instances, labelled as checks). Author adjudicates, does not review.

## 1. Where the three agree (three of three unless noted)

1. **Exact passive non-identifiability is a knife-edge, not an open set.** The observational law of (o, a) at all lags contains the policy's own independent action noise (σ_a = 0.1 in the contract), which acts as an instrument: the covariance discontinuity at the causal lag pins B separately from the smooth AR(1) confounder path. Exact E1/E2 equivalence needs σ_a = 0 in the affected actuator direction (or ρ_u = 0). Codex and Claude state this directly; Gemini's "Theorem 1" is a statement about the apparent regression coefficient of a no-latent estimator and its own remark concedes that matching the full law requires σ_a → 0. **The D-12 premise as the author wrote it, "passive support tracking is non-identifiable in principle", is false on this plant.**
2. **Non-identifiability for the model class the working claim is about is an open set.** Any predictor without a latent context variable (the frozen residual monitor and every variant of it) has an action coefficient that converges to B plus a confounding term proportional to G_b W_uᵀ; the condition for a wrong label is a strict inequality with non-empty interior (Claude: κ|G_{b,j}||W_{u,k}| > ε; Codex: nonzero cross-transfer G_b Σ_u W_uᵀ in the affected directions, not merely G_b ≠ 0 and W_u ≠ 0). More lags or more distractors do not repair it.
3. **The frozen comparator cannot distinguish actuator loss (E1) from a support-preserving confounder change (E2).** Its change score diverges as √n under both; under E2 with G_b ≠ 0 the signal is comparable to or larger than E1's and lands on channels the loss never touches; with G_b = 0 it is exactly blind to E2.
4. **Sign-randomised probes identify column k of B at the first-hit horizon irrespective of G_b, W_u and ρ_u**; the contrast goes to zero under E1, is unchanged under E2, and scales by γ under E3.
5. **The one sign-fixed prediction is the E2 × regime dissociation:** passive arm reports false loss of support under a confounder change when the confounder reaches the body, is exactly blind to it when it does not; the probe arm is invariant in both. Claude makes it the proposed primary; Codex calls it "a sharp mechanism control"; Gemini's table has the same signs.
6. **The sign of the adaptation endpoint after E1 under body confounding is not settled by derivation:** Claude predicts the passive arm still wins (less so), Codex says not sign-determined without inequalities on G_b, B, noise and paths, Gemini predicts the probe arm wins. It cannot be a preregistered primary.
7. **Finite sample.** At p = 0.05, W = 500, K = 2 there are about six units per sign per actuator; standard error of the mean contrast ≈ 0.57 σ_D, so the population advantage survives only when the body coupling is a declared fraction of the actuator column (Gemini: |G_b|/B above roughly 0.25; Codex: the projected passive bias must exceed two probe standard errors, certified per draw).
8. **Generator consequences.** The body equation gains G_b u; the certified closed loop must include u; a body ρ_min witness is needed; CL-4's isolated coordinate is no longer quiet after loss (it is driven by u), so CL-4 must be certified interventionally and crossed with G_b's support; E1b passes only if the two branches share the same u path; Codex adds a lower bound on independent action innovation so the generator cannot drift toward the knife-edge. Gemini's 35 percent radius-rejection figure is a single-model estimate, unverified.

## 2. Verdicts on option C

Claude: yes, one round, re-scoped to the E2 × regime dissociation with a latent-modelling passive third arm preregistered. Codex: not as a confirmatory freeze; a design/power pilot or a further specification pass first; the premise is false and a win could be comparator misspecification. Gemini: not now; B boxed; C only after a prototype generator passes the gate over 20 seeds with a preregistered effect size.

Disputes with round 7 accepted: option C's novelty is **3, not 3.5 to 4** (Claude, Gemini; Codex reframes the unoccupied condition as body confounding plus a restricted estimator or insufficient excitation); the D-12 flip rule "C if non-identifiable in principle" is the wrong trigger and, applied honestly, routes away from C (three of three); "easy for any second-moment detector" holds only when G_b = 0 (Gemini).

## 3. Adjudicated conclusion

Option C cannot claim an identifiability result. What it can claim is that a no-latent passive model class is systematically wrong on an open set under a body-reaching confounder while sign-randomised probing is invariant, with the E2 confuser as the clean, sign-fixed demonstration. Three models put that at the same novelty (3) as the repaired benchmark-and-findings paper of option B, with higher build cost (certification rewrite), a disputed adaptation sign, and a referee's obvious rebuttal (fit a latent state-space model) that would have to be measured as a third arm. After eight rounds, no path above a 3 has survived cross-model review. D-13 in `decisions-required.md`.
